# ALGO & ADA Wallet Upgrade and Address Change
Dear Webot Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will be upgrading the ALGO and ADA wallets starting on **July 5, 2024, at 08:00 (UTC).** The upgrade is expected to take approximately 5 minutes.

Please Note:

1. After this upgrade, the users will receive new ALGO and ADA deposit addresses. This will affect all supported tokens on the ALGO and ADA chains.

2. We recommend that the users obtain the latest deposit addresses after the maintenance period for future deposits.

3. During the upgrade, the deposit and withdrawal functions will still operate normally and will not be affected. No additional notifications will be issued upon completion of the upgrade.

4. For a certain period, deposits sent to the old addresses will still be credited until further notice.
