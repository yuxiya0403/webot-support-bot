# Webot Will Delist VET, ZEN, AR, DGB, FLOW, ICX, XVG, NEO, and PAXG
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 08:00 on June 15th, 2023 (UTC)**:

**Tokens: VET, ZEN, AR, DGB, FLOW, ICX, XVG, NEO, PAXG**

**Please note**

- This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Delisting Trading Pairs:

- **VET/USDT**

- **ZEN/USD, ZEN/USDT**

- **AR /USDT**

- **DGB/USDT**

- **FLOW/USDT**

- **ICX/USDT**

- **XVG/USDT**

- **NEO/BTC, NEO/USDT**

- **PAXG/USD, PAXG/USDT**

**
**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- **VET, ZEN, AR, DGB, FLOW, ICX, XVG, NEO, and PAXG **** **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Due to the Mainnet factors of the above-mentioned tokens, Webot will not be able to support the withdrawal features with the relevant tokens. The **VET, ZEN, AR, DGB, FLOW, ICX, XVG, NEO, and PAXG **positions will be sold simultaneously at the market price when delisting, and the basic tokens will be reissued to the users' holding accounts. If you are worried about price fluctuations, closing the bot in advance or selling them manually is recommended.

**Risk warning**

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
