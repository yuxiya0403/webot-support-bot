# Announcement on the Maintenance of Selected Trading Pairs
Dear Users,

To provide you with a better trading experience, **Webot will conduct maintenance** on several trading pairs starting at **02:00 AM (UTC) on August 21, 2025**.

During this period, trading for the following pairs will be **temporarily unavailable**:

- BTC/USDT

- DOGE/USDT

- ETH/BTC

- ETH/USDT

- SOL/BTC

- SOL/USDT

- USDC/USDT

- XRP/USDT

The maintenance is expected to take **approximately 10 minutes**. We will notify you immediately once trading is restored.

**Please Note:**
During maintenance, manual trading for these pairs will be suspended, and the opening and closing of bot orders will be paused.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and understanding.

**Risk Warning:**
There are considerable risks in digital currency transactions; please purchase carefully and pay attention to transaction risks. Webot will select high-quality currencies but will not be responsible for guarantee, compensation, etc., for any of your transactions.

If you encounter any issues, please contact our customer service team through the **online service portal** on the **Web** or **App**. Thank you for your continued support of **Webot**.
