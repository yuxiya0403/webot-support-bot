# LTC Wallet Upgrade and Address Change
Dear Users:

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will begin upgrading the **LTC wallet** on **June 18th, 2024, at 08:00 (UTC)**. The upgrade is expected to take 5 minutes.

Precautions:

1. This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th, 2022 (UTC)**.

2. After this upgrade, users will obtain new LTC deposit addresses, affecting all supported currencies on the LTC network.

3. It is recommended that users obtain a new address to deposit coins after the maintenance period.

4. During the upgrade process, related tokens' trading, deposit, and withdrawal functions will operate normally without any impact. There will be no further notification upon completion of the upgrade.

5. In the future, coins deposited to the old address will still be credited until further notice.

Risk warning:

Trading cryptocurrencies carries significant risks. Please exercise caution when purchasing and be mindful of trading risks. While Webot carefully selects quality cryptocurrencies, we do not guarantee, compensate, or assume responsibility for any of your trading activities.
