To ensure the successful processing of your wire transfer deposit, please note that:

- When transferring funds to the designated bank account of [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/), make sure that the account holder's name matches your KYC name on [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/). This is essential to avoid a rejected transaction.

- When initiating your deposit from your bank, be sure to include your **Unique Reference Code** and **Deposit Message** as outlined in the instructions. Missing these details may lead to delays in processing your deposit to [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) or, in some cases, result in a failed transaction.

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us via [service@pionex.us](mailto:service@pionex.us).
