# BTC & ETH Moon Bot
**What is BTC/ETH Moon Bot?**

BTC/ETH Moon was the first strategy introduced by Webot. Following over two years of real-market validation, it has achieved a peak return of up to 21×.

The BTC/ETH Moon strategy leverages Webot’s grid trading bot technology, with parameters intelligently recommended based on current market prices. As long as the price fluctuates within the predefined trading range, the bot operates continuously **24/7**, capturing grid profits from market movements.

These grid-generated profits can be withdrawn at any time, allowing users to maintain flexibility in how they use their funds.

**BTC/ETH Moon Parameter Introduction**

- **Total profit **= Grid profit + Unrealized profit

- **Grid Profit ** = The profit generated by the grid bot buying low and selling high

- **Trend PnL **= (Current price-purchase cost) * number of tokens of bot held

- **Grid annualized **=[(Grid profit/investment)/(Lasting time/365)]*100%

**FAQ**

**Q: What** **should I do if the bot has a loss?**
​**A:** When you create the bot, the bot will use part of the funds to buy the spots and place sell orders based on the grid. Since the bot will hold some spots, orders may fluctuate when the currency price fluctuates. But as long as you can hold it for a long time, losses will eventually become profits.

**Q: Is the fund utilization rate of the bot low?**
​**A:** Since the bot will always hold some cash for bottom hunting, these funds will not be used under normal circumstances. This also helps us manage risk so that you can move forward and backward freely in the trading market. The bot will buy for you when the currency price drops in batches.

**Q: Will the tokens selected by the bot order return to zero?**
​**A:** As for the leading coin BTC, its long-term value does not need to be repeated, while ETH is also optimistic in the long term. If you believe the blockchain will prosper, you will not need to worry about zeroing.

**Q: Can the investment amount be added or deducted after successfully creating the bot?**
​**A:** After creating the bot, you're not able to add or reduce the investment amount. You can only redistribute the investment by closing the bot, so please confirm your investment amount before creating the bot.

**Q: Can the parameters be modified while the bot is running?**
​**A:** After creating the bot, you cannot modify the bot parameters while running. You can only modify a bot by closing the bot and resetting the parameters.
