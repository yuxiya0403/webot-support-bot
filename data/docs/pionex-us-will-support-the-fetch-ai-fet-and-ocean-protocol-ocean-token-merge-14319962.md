# Pionex.US Will Support the Fetch.ai (FET) and Ocean Protocol (OCEAN) Token Merge
> Dear Users,Pionex.US will support the merge of the Fetch.ai (FET) and Ocean Protocol (OCEAN) tokens into Artificial Superintelligence (ASI...
Dear Users,

[Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will support the merge of the Fetch.ai (FET) and Ocean Protocol (OCEAN) tokens into Artificial Superintelligence (ASI).

**The specific arrangements are as follows:**

- Pionex.US stopped trading and delisted FET and OCEAN spot trading pairs at **04:00 (UTC) on June 25, 2024**, all pending orders will be canceled, and robot orders of related trading pairs will also be canceled;

- Users may refer to the [announcement](https://fetch.ai/blog/artificial-superintelligence-alliance-token-merger-approved) from the project team for more information.

**Delist trading pairs
**FET/USDT, OCEAN/USDT, OCEAN/USD

**Merge Details**

- FET and OCEAN tokens will assume the ASI ticker at Pionex.US.

- FET and OCEAN tokens will be converted to ASI tokens at the following ratios (listing times for relevant trading pairs are to be determined): 1 OCEAN = 0.433226 FET;
