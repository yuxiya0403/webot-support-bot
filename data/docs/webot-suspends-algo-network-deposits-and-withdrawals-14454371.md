# Webot suspends ALGO network deposits and withdrawals
Dear user,

In order to support ALGO upgrade, Webot has temporarily suspended the deposit and withdrawal service of ALGO(Mainnet). After the upgrade is completed, the deposit and withdrawal will be opened as soon as possible. Please understand the inconvenience caused by the suspension of the deposit and withdrawal.
