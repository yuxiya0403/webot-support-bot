# How To View USD Deposit and Withdrawal History
To view your USD deposit and withdrawal history, follow this guide:

**USD Deposit History**

To view the USD Deposit History on your mobile app and website, follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]**.

**Step 2:** Tap the **[History]** icon and select **[USD]** as your currency.

**Step 3:** Select the **[Debit Card]** filter to view your specific deposit records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241037918/813e0e29ead86dc665e10c883096/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=831ed5b10f3deffdc86162090e1a00d4c32447a984084fd997642f92784cb629&req=diIjF8l9moheUfMW3nq%2Bgcn7rscMuGpoDvYls1KQ7E3lJkFDNqTtJxqXs9Ek%0A9fsUwWepo9rhPbn2kFmekSQ1S10%3D%0A)

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to [Webot](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and navigate to **[Orders]** > **[Deposit USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select the corresponding channel on the left to view your specific deposit records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241091057/75ea62850f5669e9c44a9d0d93b3/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=e898046ca83f1af504f9d38bcd230e5dea34461f4338edc4812375c472fb5c06&req=diIjF8l3nIFaXvMW3nq%2Bgfk4XF7xL4YZx7rLVRNBdJJgubUNXqrhBbco5DiA%0ADN4voSaWrS54ky5RKQ9iJCpm8kE%3D%0A)

**USD Withdrawal History**

To view the USD Withdrawal History on your mobile app and website, please follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]** and tap on **[Withdraw]**.

**Step 2:** Tap **[History]** and check the **[USD] **page to view your withdrawal details.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241111378/86eee6cfa1d66fd8c2039e11509d/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=135dc3603d84d5324c434527c79e573fcbc3b010dc3b55c1775cdbeb8af05dcf&req=diIjF8h%2FnIJYUfMW3nq%2BgZRUae%2Fv95%2Ft1ydvR0SiW0axGAavVDTxNVXMggkX%0AFP1D1xTPFD%2Byk%2Bqyjt0%2F3rZ%2F%2FRU%3D%0A)

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to the Webot website and go to **[Orders]** > **[Withdraw USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select your preferred withdrawal method to view the specific transaction history.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241124413/9914cb75a8678fbc0ea3507f7845/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=6cb496995004bdb05d7aba29c78c1977d78b54dfe16bcf916c0057c15a899c84&req=diIjF8h8mYVeWvMW3nq%2BgV%2Fkv3iL%2B68%2FjhJzIh5bhpZKOCmP7odYAENw1XVR%0AGMKX3YLU529M2teSjagQl%2Br333Y%3D%0A)
