You can check your Wire deposit's "Withdrawable Time" by following the steps.

👉🏻 **[App operation step](#h_01H36Y6VNAYMMJN2H5252HQNRQ)**

**👉🏻 [Web operation step](#h_01H36Y708B76GXSK46RMAHXGHJ)**

Mobile App operation step

Please click **[Home] **-** [Deposit] **-** [History] **-** [Wire] **to check the **[****Withdrawable Time]**.

​​​​

![](https://static.intercomassets.com/assets/article_broken_image_placeholder.png)

Website operation step

1. After logging in to the account, click **[Wallet]** - **[Overview]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177995777/524559d86c7f0099f8523be47e49/19722443419545?expires=1776232800&signature=6e27e6830981093025e4bd416eb156a2cb8acd90a4b2bf39a16d38a78a3d01ac&req=diEgEcB3mIZYXvMW3nq%2BgekrQjaH1QjNtpaFoQg9vxbv5ukMrtK4paIow59V%0AuCFuziufZSJXYlBRwakkZ%2BxZvPk%3D%0A)

2. Click **[USD]** under the **"Deposit"** tab.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177995819/0a7edc8e2e41a5a71cff97af7f6c/19722443421209?expires=1776232800&signature=63ce1394f5b0ab25acebbea345344473afabac823d5ab6e365a3c7f86d6af326&req=diEgEcB3mIleUPMW3nq%2BgUjexPoczAjMbH%2BJyxL5mKRy0t76AujETAZFs9oG%0AScG3iXG8vD%2F%2B%2BcPHYiRPXAF2y8E%3D%0A)

3. In the **"Wire Transfer"** tab, click **[Order History]** - **[Deposit History]** - **[Withdrawable Time]**.
