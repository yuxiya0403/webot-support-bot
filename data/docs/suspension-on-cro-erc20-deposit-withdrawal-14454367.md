# Suspension on CRO(ERC20) Deposit & Withdrawal
Dear users,

Webot will have maintenance for CRO(ERC20) deposit and withdrawal. After the maintenance is completed, it will be reopened. We apologize for any inconvenience during the maintenance. 

**Please note:**

- Any CRO deposits will not be credited after CRO(ERC20) deposits and withdrawals are suspended.

- If users had a CRO(ERC20) deposit launching before the announcement and not being credited to Webot account, users could contact Webot customer service for a token refund.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously.
