# ADA Wallet Upgrade
Dear Webot Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will be upgrading the ADA wallets starting on **September 1, 2024, at 20:30 (UTC)**.

Please Note:
1. During the upgrade and maintenance period, the trading functions of the related tokens will remain unaffected.
2. During the upgrade and maintenance period, the deposit and withdrawal functions of ADA will be temporarily suspended. Once the upgrade is complete, these functions will be automatically restored without further notice.

Risk Warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously.
