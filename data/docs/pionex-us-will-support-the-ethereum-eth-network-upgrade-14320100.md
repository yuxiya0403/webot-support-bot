Dear users,

Pionex.US will support the Ethereum (ETH) network upgrade.

- The Ethereum (ETH) network upgrade will take place at the [Ethereum block height of 15,050,000](https://etherscan.io/block/countdown/15050000), or approximately at 2022-06-29 10:43(UTC). Deposits and withdrawals of ETH and ERC-20 tokens will be suspended starting from 2022-06-29 09:30(UTC).

Please note:

- The trading of ETH and ERC-20 tokens will not be affected during the network upgrade.

- The Ethereum (ETH) network upgrade will occur at the [Ethereum block height of 15,050,000](https://etherscan.io/block/countdown/15050000). The estimated time is for users’ reference only.

- We will reopen deposits and withdrawals for ETH and ERC-20 tokens once we deem the upgraded network to be stable, and we will not notify users in a further announcement.
