Dear users,

Pionex.US will suspend the deposit and withdrawal services related to FIL (Filecoin) at 13:00 on March 14, 2023 (UTC) to support FIL (Filecoin) at [Epoch 2,683,348](https://filfox.info/en) — expected to upgrade network at 15:15 on March 14, 2023 (UTC).

**Please noted:**

- During the FIL (Filecoin) network upgrade period, FIL-related transactions will not be affected;

- Please handle FIL (Filecoin)-related deposits and withdrawals in advance. During the deposit and withdrawal maintenance period, FIL (Filecoin) deposits will not be credited;

- As the FIL (Filecoin) network upgrade is complete, Pionex.US will reopen the deposit and withdrawal service of FIL (Filecoin) when the network is stable;
