# Webot will support StormX(STMX) smart contract replacement
Dear users,

Webot will support StormX(STMX) ERC20 smart contract replacement. Further details about the replacement are shown below:

- The deposit and withdrawal of STMX(ERC20) will be suspended;

- During the smart contract replacement period, the STMX spot trading will not be affected;

- After the StormX(STMX) ERC20 smart contract replacement is completed, we will notify users in a further announcement.

**For further information:**

- New STMX(ERC20) smart contract address: [0xa62cc35625b0c8dc1faea39d33625bb4c15bd71c](https://etherscan.io/token/0xa62cc35625b0c8dc1faea39d33625bb4c15bd71c)

- [STMX Token Contract Swap](https://stormx.io/blog/stmx-token-swap)
