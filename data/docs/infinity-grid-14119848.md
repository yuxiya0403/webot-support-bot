Why do you need Infinity Grid

The Infinity Grid Bot is a **premium version of our Grid Trading Bot**. With the Infinity Grid Bot, you never have to worry about the price exceeding your grid range. It operates 24/7 to help you “buy low and sell high,” ensuring that the total value of your assets remains consistent even as the price continues to rise.

When to Start Using the Infinity Grid Bot

When the market begins to show an upward trend—especially at the start of a bull market—running the Infinity Grid Bot becomes a powerful tool for earning USDT. This makes the Infinity Grid Bot a strong candidate for long-term use as well.

Example for using an Infinity Grid?

Let’s say you have 500 USDT and want to create an Infinity Grid Bot for the BTC/USDT pair, with the current price of Bitcoin at 10,000 USDT. For this example, let’s use the following parameters:

- Lowest price: 9,000 USDT

- Profit per grid: 1%

- Total investment: 500 USDT

Once started, the bot will buy some BTC — approximately 450 USDT worth. It will keep around 50 USDT in reserve in case the price drops, since the lowest price is set below the current price. The bot will then ensure that your BTC holdings always maintain a value of around 450 USDT. In our example, this means:

- Once the price rises by 1% and reaches 10,100 USDT, the value of your BTC holdings will increase to 454.5 USDT.

- The bot will then sell a portion of BTC—specifically 4.5 USDT worth—so that the remaining BTC still holds a value of 450 USDT.

- As a result, your BTC value remains stable at 450 USDT, and your USDT balance in the bot increases to 50 + 4.5 = 54.5 USDT.

When the price rises another 1%, the bot will sell a small amount of BTC again to keep your BTC value at 450 USDT. So, you'll still have BTC worth 450 USDT plus 60.05 USDT in cash! Once the price starts to fall again, the opposite will happen—the bot will buy more BTC to maintain that same 450 USDT value.

In short: No matter how the price moves, the Infinity Grid Bot ensures your BTC value in USDT remains constant, and with every price fluctuation, you earn a small profit. The bot will keep doing this infinitely, as long as the price stays above your set lowest price.

Sounds good, right?

Bot Interface Parameters Meaning

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177938441/198a26b7a3309c941e5389210db9/49162868690713?expires=1776232800&signature=a932a914afe190b291d91a6f6d1e5cbe54ff1f2a44cb5f6c42a68d46db0efd79&req=diEgEcB9lYVbWPMW3nq%2BgZe1CZZb1atKQYGVfsLEVDF%2FGCt8r0K9ayD0tEik%0A%2Ff51ry22NpicFgisBhmmrTMBgcY%3D%0A)

- **Investment:** The funds invested in the running bot

- **Total profit:** Grid profit + Unrealized profit

- **Unrealized profit:** (Current price-average buying price) * number of coins held

- **Grid profit:** Grid bot buys low and sells high and makes profits from arbitrage

- **Grid annualized:** [(Grid profit/investment)/(Lasting time/365)]*100%

- **Total annualized:** [(Total profit/investment)/(Lasting time/365)]*100%
