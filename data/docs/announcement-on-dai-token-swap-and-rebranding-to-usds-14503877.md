# Announcement on DAI Token Swap and Rebranding to USDS
> Announcement about DAI token swap to USDS
Dear Users,

Webot will support the token swap and rebranding of **DAI** to **USDS**. Accordingly, the following adjustments will be made to DAI-related features:

**1. Delisting of Spot Trading Pairs**
At **02:00 (UTC) on April 10th, 2026**, DAI/USD spot trading pair will be discontinued and delisted. All open orders and related bot orders will be canceled.

**2. Suspension of Deposit and Withdrawal Services**
DAI deposits and withdrawals have been suspended.

**3. DAI Position Snapshot and Token Swap**
After the trading pairs are delisted, a snapshot of users holding DAI will be taken. Eligible users will receive FRAX tokens at a **1 DAI = 1 USDS** ratio.

**4. Reopening of USDS Trading**
The USDS/USD and USDS/USDT trading pair is scheduled to resume trading at approximately **08:00 (UTC) on April 10, 2026**.

**Risk Warning:**

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot cannot assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thank you for your continued support of Webot!
