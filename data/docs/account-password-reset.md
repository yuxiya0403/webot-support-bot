You can reset your account password from the Webot web or app. _**Please note that for security reasons, withdrawals from your account will be suspended for 24 hours after the password reset.**_

👉🏻 [**App operation step**](#h_01KFJJHPFKESKGAR7R3AZS2030)

👉🏻 [**Web operation step**](#h_01KFJJHPFQZEYKJM7F43WF1NHP)

App operation step

**Step 1:** Open the [Webot app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download), go to **[Account]** - **[Forgot password?]**. After reading the prompt, click **[Continue]**.

_

**Step 2:** Enter the email associated with your Webot account, and click **[Next step]**. Complete the CAPTCHA verification, then enter the Google Authenticator verification code and click **[Confirm]**.

_

**Step 3**: Next, enter the email/SMS verification code and click **[Confirm]**. Enter your new password, re-enter the same password, and finally click **[Next step]** to complete the setup successfully.

_

Web operation step

**Step 1:** Go to the [Webot website](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and click **[Sign in]** - **[Forgot password?]**.

_

**Step 2:** Enter the phone number or email associated with your Webot account, click **[Next],** and complete the CAPTCHA verification.

_

**Step 3: **Enter the Google Authenticator verification code and click **[Confirm]**. Next, enter the email/SMS verification code and click **[Confirm]** again to continue.

_

**Step 4:** Enter your new password, re-enter the same password, and finally click **[Submit]** to complete the setup successfully.

_

FAQ

**Q： I'm having trouble receiving Email/SMS verification codes. How can I resolve this?**

A： Please refer to the following appropriate guides to resolve the issues: [Can't Receive Email Verification Code](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868734406671), [Can't Receive SMS Verification Code](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868697344143).

**Q： The system shows ‘invalid verification code’ when entering the email/SMS code. How to resolve?**

A： Please refer to the [Email/SMS Verification Codes Wrong](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868710828687) article for troubleshooting.

**Q： What should I do if I get a Google Authenticator code error when I reset my password? **

A： Please refer to the [2FA Code Error article](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14900368289807) for troubleshooting.

**Q： What should I do if I lose Google Authenticator and can't reset my password? **

A： Please follow the instructions in the [Steps to Manually Reset Google Authenticator](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868722763151) article to reset your Google Authenticator first. Once the reset is successful, you can reset your password.

Please get in touch with [Webot Support](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) if you have any other questions.
