# Webot Will Delist Some Trading Pairs
Dear users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 07:00 on April 12th, 2023 (UTC)**:

- **BCH, OXT, BSV, DASH, UMA, ARPA, BOND, DAI, NFT, REQ, SRM, STMX**

**Please note**

- Delisting trading pairs: **BCH/USD, BCH/USDT, BCH/BTC, OXT/USD, OXT/USDT, OXT/BTC, BSV/USDT, BSV/BTC, DASH/USDT, DASH/BTC, UMA/USDT, UMA/BTC, ARPA/USDT, BOND/USDT, DAI/USDT, NFT/USDT, REQ/USDT, SRM/BUSD, STMX/USDT**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- The** BCH, OXT, BSV, DASH, UMA, ARPA, BOND, DAI, NFT, REQ, SRM, and STMX **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Webot will support the withdrawal features of the tokens mentioned above after delisting.

**Risk warning**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thanks for your support!
