# Announcement on Upgrading Polygon (MATIC) Token to Polygon (POL)
Dear Users,

According to upgrading Polygon(MATIC) Token to Polygon(POL), We will delist the MATIC/USD, MATIC/USDT and MATIC/BTC trading pair on September 9, 2024 at 9:00(UTC+0)

We will list the trading pairs POL/USD, POL/USDT and POL/BTC on September 13, 2024 at 12:30(UTC+0).

Please Note:

Trading pairs to be removed: MATIC/USD, MATIC/USDT and MATIC/BTC.

After trading stops, trading bot orders and manual orders for related trading pairs will be canceled, but orders containing MATIC assets in the Rebalancing Bot will not be canceled, and the MATIC holdings in the Rebalancing Bot will be released.

Webot will support the MATIC replacement plan. The specific arrangements are as follows:

Webot will adopt Polygon (POL)as the token code for Polygon (MATIC) .
All Polygon (MATIC) tokens will be replaced with Polygon (POL) tokens at a ratio of 1 Polygon (MATIC) = 1 Polygon (POL).
Webot will assist users who hold MATIC tokens in their Webot accounts to handle any technical issues involved.

Risk Warning:
Trading digital assets involve significant risks. Please purchase cautiously and be aware of the trading risks. Webot will select high-quality tokens but does not bear any guarantee or compensation responsibility for your trading behavior.

If you encounter any issues, please contact our customer service through the WEB or APP online customer service entrance in a timely manner. Thank you for your continuous support of Webot!
