# Why Does It Show "0" For Withdrawable Funds?
Have you noticed a discrepancy between your Total Balance and your Withdrawable Balance? Common issues include seeing a "0" balance or being unable to withdraw the full amount of your holdings.

These restrictions are typically triggered by security protocols, active trading positions, or the specific asset type you are holding. Please review the following scenarios to resolve the issue.

**1. Mandatory Fiat Deposit Holding Periods**

To comply with risk management protocols, deposits via fiat channels are subject to a temporary holding period. During this time, the equivalent value of your deposit (in either USD or crypto) cannot be withdrawn.

**Deposit Method**

**Holding Period**

**Debit Card**

**10 Days**

**ACH Transfer**

**14 Days**

**Wire Transfer**

**24 Hours**

**How to check availability:**

- **Web:** Navigate to **Orders** > **Deposit USD** **orders**. Select the corresponding fiat deposit channel and refer to the “Withdrawable Time” column.

- **App:** Go to **Home** > **Deposit** > **History**. Switch to the corresponding fiat deposit channel and check the “Withdrawable Time” for each transaction.

**2. Asset Type Mismatch**

If you are attempting to withdraw to a bank account, your funds must be held in **USD**. If your balance consists of tokens (e.g., BTC, ETH), your "USD Withdrawable" balance will appear as zero.

You must convert your digital assets into USD using the **[Convert](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119731-how-to-convert-cryptocurrency)** function. Once converted, the USD balance will be eligible for withdrawal to your bank account.

**3. Funds Locked in Trading Bots**

Assets currently allocated to an active Trading Bot are reserved for its strategy and are not available for withdrawal.

**How to release funds:**

- **Web:** Go to **Orders** > **Trading Bot orders** > **Running orders** > click **More** > select **Stop the Bot. **Choose whether to sell your holdings or keep them as-is, then click **Confirm**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2237965129/2b385208fc1ba6e668ee7d92a3d4/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=14f1c93f760a7da2e51d5e6eda10c90f3fe8acb99691260562106fe571d54462&req=diIkEcB4mIBdUPMW3nq%2BgSxGxm44%2FhO7%2ByahWK2tcFnXoBqbW4Y1txs6VQsN%0A4UOVmxVEs5KTNpblQOpWF1ILIV8%3D%0A)

- **App:** Navigate to the **Bot** tab > Select the bot order > Tap the **three-line icon** > click **More** > **Close the Bot**. Choose whether to sell your holdings or keep them as-is, then click **Confirm**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2238009241/daf7ef9ba48b46583044d6cea9a9/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=f6e8468e6d71ea27a756114ff192d73a6dcacf449a8a1003e937c8b720fd43f4&req=diIkHsl%2BlINbWPMW3nq%2BgYzEXQRJwHBR%2FHRrgAmn75eE1OU4k3M4zZiro%2BER%0AbDlxVTIhKtc1Qe68B7bHbMYIf3M%3D%0A)

**4. Pending Limit Orders**

When you place a Limit Order that remains unfilled, the system "locks" the required funds to ensure the trade can be executed if your target price is reached.

**How to release funds:**

- **Web:** Go to **Orders** > **Manual** **orders** > **Placed orders** > Locate the order and click **Cancel** > **Submit**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2238033216/c861b590e54f261a3a07ae7686f3/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=9785ae74c3096799c60b66ac0504488a924a66d43268a191e61f737b30725e68&req=diIkHsl9noNeX%2FMW3nq%2BgTEf1RV8bZsU%2FtDIH86lp3DGoiLBcHTwSyGldLYU%0A1OkpYeOZe3OEHp70OuQVcGxnylM%3D%0A)

- **App:** Go to the **Account** page > Select **USDT** > Under the "Filled Orders" section, tap **View all** > Switch to the **Placed** tab > Locate the order and tap **Cancel** > **Confirm**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2238048073/0ece55f493c1bfbe794d2ef53842/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=ba0d67525dbc172c89819cd5be84c4a7eeb7e6486dde70cbffabfcc8375b5eea&req=diIkHsl6lYFYWvMW3nq%2BgX9IaYBY0oZckc9wBHz7o3UytuOFaPXsiV%2FX6R0Q%0AXstWhAnpTcfyWMVDdIKArTAfS2A%3D%0A)

**Still need assistance?** If your funds remain unavailable after checking these scenarios, please contact Webot Support for further assistance.
