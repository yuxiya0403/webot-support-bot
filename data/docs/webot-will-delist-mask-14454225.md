# Webot Will Delist MASK
Dear Users,

​

To enhance market liquidity and improve the overall trading experience, Webot will delist the following trading pair at 2:00 am UTC on April 13, 2025:

- MASK/USD

Please Note:

- 

All bot orders and manual orders associated with the above trading pair will be automatically canceled at the time of delisting.

- 

MASK tokens tied to bot orders will be released to your trading account once those orders are canceled.

- 

Trading for MASK will remain available via the MASK/USDT pair, and users may continue to trade or create bots through this pair.

- 

Any trading fees incurred from closing existing MASK/USD bots and opening MASK/USDT bots will be compensated accordingly.

Risk Warning

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
