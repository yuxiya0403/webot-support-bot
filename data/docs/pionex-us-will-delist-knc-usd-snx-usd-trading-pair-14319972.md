# Pionex.US Will Delist KNC/USD,SNX/USD Trading Pair
> Dear Users,Due to the poor liquidity of  KNC/USD, SNX/USD, Pionex.US will delist the  KNC/USD, SNX/USD trading pair at 02:30 on October 20...
Dear Users,

Due to the poor liquidity of** KNC/USD, SNX/USD**, Pionex.US will delist the **KNC/USD, SNX/USD **trading pair** at 02:30 on October 20th, 2023 (UTC)**. Users can trade **KNC, and SNX** with the **KNC/USDT， and SNX/USDT ** trading pair.

**Please Note**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Pionex.US will delist the **KNC/USD, SNX/USD** trading pair and cancel the related manual orders and bot orders, and the **KNC, SNX **positions will be released to the trading account.

- Users can trade **KNC, SNX** with the **KNC/USDT, and SNX/USDT ** trading pair after delisting the **KNC/USD, SNX/USD** trading pair.
