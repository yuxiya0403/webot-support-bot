# How to View Crypto Deposit and Withdrawal History
To view Crypto deposit and withdrawal history, follow this guide:

👉🏻**[Crypto Deposit History](#h_01H5HPWBBNR96R10RYM08G6AXN)**

👉🏻 **[Crypto Withdrawal History](#h_01H5HPWKDP2ECBFCRAXA3QJBQG)**

Crypto Deposit History

To view the Crypto Deposit History on your mobile app and website, follow these steps:

Mobile App:

1. On the homepage, click **[Deposit]** and select the **[History]** tab on the right.

2. On the **"Deposit"** page, switch to the **[Crypto]** tab to view your deposit records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2181500920/0447a58429c946cff7f75aa3445d/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=bf3b8720544e83c4b7373e03e57d5f6ae53cd30c06be7f0fcf0f90f385a0b372&req=diEvF8x%2BnYhdWfMW3nq%2BgbPDnGVylzcYaEhlBdFAi86J2SIlkXeqmYaWQRs1%0AwsvUBnQq%2FDy3wQPbNrh5yYBy0Wg%3D%0A)

Website:

1. Log in to your [Webot web](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/balance/deposit) account and click **[Wallet]** on the top menu.

2. Select **[Receive Crypto]** to view your History.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2181524503/2a787b61f69fa91771ef8599144c/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=5270f17c291cd070d5c058b487500be98c68580d7151198d352a9355aae85304&req=diEvF8x8mYRfWvMW3nq%2BgaZjVI7MXBV4gBVEO4Mmgv8KJPsdA2HNbGMAznuK%0Ail0ME3O4uzfRveySdVIBM%2BVTwc8%3D%0A)

Crypto Withdrawal History

To view the Crypto Withdrawal History on your mobile app and website, please follow these steps:

Mobile App:

1. On the homepage, click **[Deposit]**, switch to **[Withdraw]**, and select the **[History]** tab on the right.

2. On the **"Withdraw"** page, switch to the **[Crypto]** tab to view your withdrawal records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2181541763/de7e9daf3838a0d8e8de56663a79/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=ece0ca98d473232a477c5ad15525b8cb554818731a773366ade588d05428d580&req=diEvF8x6nIZZWvMW3nq%2BgVI6C6wfbzDCwJQbG6zqF6brA3Uoj0PGtnmoeOkZ%0AEGjf1hF54xn%2Fo1pHX%2FB0G4fhKt4%3D%0A)

Website:

1. Log in to your [Webot web](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/balance/withdraw) account and click **[Wallet]** on the top menu.

2. Select **[Send Crypto]** to view your History.
