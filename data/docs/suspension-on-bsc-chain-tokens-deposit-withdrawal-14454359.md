# Suspension on BSC Chain Tokens Deposit & Withdrawal
Dear users, 

Due to the BSC cross-chain bridge being attacked, Webot has suspended the deposit and withdrawal of BSC Chain tokens. After the emergent upgrade is completed, it will be reopened. We apologize for any inconvenience during the suspension.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously.
