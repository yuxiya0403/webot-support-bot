# How to Receive Crypto on Webot
If you own cryptocurrency on another platform or wallet, you can transfer it to your Webot account by following these steps.

👉🏻 **[App operation step](#h_01JXGXXEJZRR0FN2PQ0E0EH01P)**

👉🏻 **[Web operation step](#h_01JXGXXEK9F66XB0D15K1CF1JV)**

**App operation step**

**Step 1:** After logging in, click **[Home]** - **[Deposit]** - **[Deposit]** - **[Receive from exchange/wallet]**.

_

**Step 2:** Search for the cryptocurrency you wish to deposit, and select it. You’ll see the available networks based on the cryptocurrency you choose.
​
​_*Please select the network carefully and ensure it matches the network of the platform or wallet from which you are withdrawing funds. If you choose the wrong network, your funds may be lost and cannot be recovered._

_

**Step 3:** You can obtain the relevant deposit address for the network you chose by clicking the **[Copy]** button. Then, paste the copied address into the address field on the platform from which you intend to withdraw cryptocurrency.

_

**Step 4:** After the blockchain confirms the deposit request, your transfer will be processed. The confirmation time varies depending on the blockchain and its current network traffic.

**Step 5:** The deposit will be credited to your Webot Account, and you can check the deposit record on the Crypto Deposit History page.

_

**Web operation step**

**Step 1: **After logging into your [Webot account](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/sign), click **[Wallet]** - **[Receive Crypto]** - **[Receive Crypto].**

_

**Step 2:** Click the dropdown arrow, search for the cryptocurrency you wish to deposit, and select it.

_

**Step 3:** You’ll see the available networks based on the cryptocurrency you choose. 
​
​_*Please select the network carefully and ensure it matches the network of the platform or wallet from which you are withdrawing funds. If you choose the wrong network, your funds may be lost and cannot be recovered._

_

**Step 4:** You can obtain the relevant deposit address for the network you chose by clicking the **[Copy]** button. Then, paste the copied address into the address field on the platform from which you intend to withdraw cryptocurrency.

_

**Step 5:** After the blockchain confirms the deposit request, your transfer will be processed. The confirmation time varies depending on the blockchain and its current network traffic.

**Step 6:** The deposit will be credited to your Webot Account, and you can check the deposit record on the Crypto Deposit History page.

_

**Notes**

- Please deposit only supported cryptocurrencies using the supported networks listed on the [cryptocurrencies that support deposits and withdrawals](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/fees).

- Each cryptocurrency has a [minimum deposit amount](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/fees), which depends on the cryptocurrency type and network. Any deposits below the minimum will not be credited or refunded. Please make sure to account for transaction fees before making a withdrawal from another platform or wallet.

- For cryptocurrency deposits that require a memo or tag, please ensure you fill in this information correctly. You can find it in the deposit window. Depositing without a memo or tag, or using an incorrect memo or tag, may result in the deposit process not being completed smoothly. To potentially recover such assets, please get in touch with Webot Support.

**FAQ**

**Q1: I deposited via the wrong chain, used the wrong currency, or my deposit has not arrived. Can I recover my funds?**
​**A:** After logging in, please go to the "[Self-service recovery](https://www.webot.com/en-US/deposit/retrieve)" page. Then choose **[Deposit the wrong chain, the currency has not arrived]** - **[[Fill out the form to retrieve](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfUFDaOj2p4gFuIx2rbi2vrn1Yyr97s_7rpU2X-QLAC1dAHYA/viewform)]** and complete the form. After we receive your submission, we will contact you within 3 business days.

**Q2: Deposited to the old wallet address. Can I recover my funds?**
​**A:** After logging in, please go to the "[Self-service recovery](https://www.webot.com/en-US/deposit/retrieve)" page. Then choose **[Deposit to the old wallet address]** - **[Deposit to the old wallet address]** - **[Retrieve Now]** to complete the process.

**Q3: Why hasn’t my deposit arrived, even if the address and network are correct?**

**A:** Deposits may be delayed due to pending blockchain confirmations or a slight synchronization lag between the network and our platform.

If your transaction is marked as "Success" on the blockchain explorer but hasn't appeared in your account, please contact Webot Support with your transaction hash (TXID) for assistance.

**Q4: What is a contract address, and can I deposit funds into it?**
​**A:** Since different cryptocurrencies can have similar names or tickers, Webot provides the contract address that uniquely identifies each token, preventing confusion and ensuring that the correct token is deposited. 

However, please note that users should only deposit cryptocurrencies into the specific wallet address that is shown on your deposit page. 

*_Depositing cryptocurrencies into the contract address may result in asset losses._

**Q5: Can I deposit money to Webot through any address or platform?**
​**A:** If your funds come from gambling or fraudulent platforms, your deposit will undergo an extended risk control review. Do not deposit funds to Webot from sanctioned or high-risk entities, as this may result in restrictions on your account.

**Q6: Why is my deposit still pending or not yet credited?**

**A:** If your deposit is under review, it may have triggered our risk control system for routine security verification. Please complete the manual verification form to expedite the process:

Go to **[Deposit]** — **[History]**, and click **[Please submit additional information]**.

**Q7: Why is my smart contract deposit not showing up?**

A: Webot currently does not support automatic crediting for smart contract deposits on certain tokens and networks. If your deposit was executed through a smart contract, please contact Webot Support and provide your transaction hash (TXID) for manual assistance.

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
