**Notice:**

- Spot Trading will be suspended, and the creating and canceling of trading bot orders on SNX/USDT will be suspended.

- During the maintenance, there might be risks in Spot-Futures Arbitrage Bot (The futures position cannot be hedged during the maintenance). If you are concerned about price fluctuation during the maintenance, it is recommended to close Spot-Futures Arbitrage Bot in advance to avoid losses due to price fluctuations.

- Transactions and cryptocurrencies deposits and withdrawals of other trading pairs will not be affected.
