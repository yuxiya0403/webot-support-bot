# How Do I Add Funds To The Running Grid Bot?
If you want to add funds to the running grid bot, please follow the steps in the image below.

**👉🏻 [Web operation step](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868810689935/live_preview/01KMFBGDGSG9A9YTMBT7D5V5K0#h_01H36YA6GP7FHYY399YPPR81D3)**

👉🏻 **[App operation step](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868810689935/live_preview/01KMFBGDGSG9A9YTMBT7D5V5K0#h_01KMFB661H2JB9V4CM6J8DNS42)**

**Web operation step**

Open the [Webot webpage](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and log in to your account, then click **[Orders]** - ** [Trading Bot Orders]** - **[Running orders]** - **[Add investment]**, enter the amount of money you want to add, and click **[OK]**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2194768555/76b883ba291abb5d48e0e143572b/1.png?expires=1776232800&signature=611f8345ff6b419fef356d42e1d35b0c46f2d7ebb575bb20bcef975de6378997&req=diEuEs54lYRaXPMW3nq%2Bgab3B3nR6PVstzbzFp30Y6HNRtvABQRe9WJ4wuiz%0A1aSMSZYV%2BhgNNAIPd1S2BPR2ikc%3D%0A)

**App Operation Steps**

Open the [app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download) and log in to your account. Click **[Bot]**, find the bot order, and click **[Detail]** - **[More]** - **[Add investment]** in order, drag the slider, enter the added amount, and click **[Confirm]**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2194769843/3ccc38be121277d970dddd2ee7e7/2.png?expires=1776232800&signature=ec02d2bbb699ad0bf8e10d542531653a081ab8ee0df13a3986e797d6cb80d369&req=diEuEs54lIlbWvMW3nq%2BgdYXNFpElonlaHsejidasMkS78qRwzQIPqvoUgBS%0Akt%2FYGY9hIDicjq%2Bewa76HB0HqoA%3D%0A)
