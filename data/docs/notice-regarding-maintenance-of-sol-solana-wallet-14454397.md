# Notice Regarding Maintenance of SOL (SOLANA) Wallet
Dear Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will begin upgrading the **SOL (SOLANA)**** wallet on April 23rd, 2024, at 08:00 (UTC). The expected upgrade time is 5 minutes.**

Please note:

**1. This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).
2. After this upgrade, users will receive a new SOL deposit address, affecting all supported currencies on the SOLANA chain.
****3. After this upgrade, we recommend users obtain the new address for depositing.
**4. During the upgrade, deposit and withdrawal functions will operate normally and will not be affected. There will be no further notification upon completion of the upgrade.
5. For a while after the upgrade, deposits to the old address will still be credited until further notice.

Risk warning:
Trading cryptocurrencies carries significant risks. Please exercise caution when purchasing and be mindful of trading risks. While Webot carefully selects quality cryptocurrencies, we do not guarantee, compensate, or assume responsibility for any of your trading activities.
