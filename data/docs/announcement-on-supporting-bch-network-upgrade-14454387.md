# Announcement on Supporting BCH Network Upgrade
Dear users,

**Due to the BCH network upgrade**, Webot is expected to suspend the deposit and withdrawal of the BCH network **at 09:30 on May 15th, 2023 (UTC)**.

**Please Note
**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- During the network upgrade, the trading functions of BCH related tokens will not be affected.

- During the deposit and withdrawal maintenance period of BCH, the deposit of relevant tokens will not be credited.

- After the network upgrade, Webot will reopen the deposit and withdrawal functions of the BCH network when the network is stable.

Thank you for your continuous support to Webot!

Webot Team
