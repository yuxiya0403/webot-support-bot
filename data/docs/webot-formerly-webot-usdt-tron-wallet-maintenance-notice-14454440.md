# Webot (formerly Webot) USDT(Tron) Wallet Maintenance Notice
Dear Users,

To provide you with a better experience, Webot (formerly [Webot](http://webot.com)) has temporarily suspended USDT (TRON) deposits and withdrawals, effective 01:00 UTC on April 1, 2026.

**Please Note:**

- Users can continue to trade normally during the wallet maintenance.

- Any deposits made during the maintenance will not be credited automatically, they will be processed and credited gradually once the maintenance is completed.

- After the upgrade is completed and the network is stable, deposit and withdrawal services will be reopened without further notice.
