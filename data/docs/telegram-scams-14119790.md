# Telegram Scams
Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible. If you send cryptocurrency to a third party, you can't reverse it or stop payment. When you send cryptocurrency to a blockchain address, you must be certain of the legitimacy of any involved third-party services and only send cryptocurrency to entities you trust.

Webot has no official support on the Telegram messaging platform, and Webot community admins will never message users first. Various types of scams on Telegram target Webot community members, including fraudulent payment bots and giveaway scams.

**Pro tip:** If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is a scam.
