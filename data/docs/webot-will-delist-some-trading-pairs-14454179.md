# Webot Will Delist Some Trading Pairs
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the following trading pairs **at 08:00 on October 17th, 2023 (UTC)**:

- **QTUM/ETH**

- **SUSHI/BTC**

- **REN/BTC**

- **RLC/BTC**

- **GRT/BTC**

- **KNC/BTC**

- **OCEAN/BTC**

**Please note:**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- The positions of the relevant tokens mentioned above in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Users can trade the assets mentioned above with other trading pairs.

**Risk warning:**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thanks for your support!
