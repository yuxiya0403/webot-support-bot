# Announcement on Supporting ETH (Ethereum) Network Shapella Upgrade
Dear Users,

Webot will support the Shapella upgrade of the ETH (Ethereum) network. The specific arrangements are as follows:

- Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal functions of ETH (Ethereum), OP (Optimism), and ARB (Arbitrum) related network tokens at 16:00 on April 12th, 2023 (UTC), including ETH-Optimism, ETH-Arbitrum, ETH, and ETH network-related tokens to support the Shapella upgrade of the ETH (Ethereum) network will be performed at [epoch 194,048](https://beaconcha.in/) — expected at 22:27 on April 12th, 2023 (UTC).

Please Note

- During the network upgrade of ETH (Ethereum), OP (Optimism), and ARB (Arbitrum), the trading functions of related network tokens will not be affected.

- Please handle the deposit and withdrawal requirements of relevant network tokens in advance. During the network upgrade, the deposit of relevant network tokens will not be credited.

- After upgrading the Ethereum (ETH) network, Webot will reopen the deposit and withdrawal functions of ETH, OP, and ARB network-related tokens when the networks are stable.

More information:

- [ETH Mainnet Shapella Announcement](https://blog.ethereum.org/2023/03/28/shapella-mainnet-announcement)

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will do its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
