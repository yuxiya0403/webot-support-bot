# Temporary Maintenance on the Deposit and Withdrawal Features
Dear User,

To provide better service, Webot will upgrade and maintain the deposit and withdrawal features **at 22:30 on October 17th, 2023 (UTC)**. During this period, all deposit and withdrawal functions will be closed.

Please Note:

- The maintenance is expected to last for **300** minutes.

- Webot will resume the deposit and withdrawal features after the completion of the maintenance, and there will be no further notice.
