# How to Buy Crypto with the "Buy Crypto" Feature
> The Buy Crypto feature allows you to easily buy cryptocurrencies on the Webot platform in just seconds. It offers a streamlined alternativ...
The [**Buy Crypto **](https://www.webot.com/en-US/buy-crypto/buy)feature allows you to easily **buy **cryptocurrencies on the Webot platform in just _seconds_. It offers a streamlined _alternative_ to the Webot [trade screen](https://www.webot.com/en-US/trade/BTC_USD/Manual) for those who don't require advanced options. Read on to learn how to buy your favorite cryptocurrencies in just a few easy steps below.

_*Please Note: Not all cryptocurrencies [supported](https://www.webot.com/en-US/market/spot) by Webot are available using the **Buy Crypto** feature. If your cryptocurrency is supported by Webot but not visible in the **Buy Crypto** section, please use the regular Webot [trade screen](https://www.webot.com/en-US/trade/BTC_USD/Manual). _
