# Webot on social media
For direct support, please click** [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119748-how-to-contact-webot-support)**. To stay safe and informed, please ensure you are following our official verified accounts. Webot is exclusively represented by the following communities:

**Twitter**

- [@Webot](https://twitter.com/PionexUS)

**Telegram**

- [Webot Official](https://t.me/pionexus)

**Discord**

- [Webot Official](https://discord.gg/dZbvbeXK46)

⚠️ **Security & Anti-Scam Notice**

To ensure your asset safety, please keep the following in mind:

- **No PMs First:** Webot administrators and support staff will **NEVER** direct message (DM) you first on Telegram or any other social platform.

- **No Financial Transactions:** Official staff will **NEVER** ask for payments, seed phrases, or transfer of funds via social media.

- **Official Support:** For account-related issues, please only use our Official Support Link.
