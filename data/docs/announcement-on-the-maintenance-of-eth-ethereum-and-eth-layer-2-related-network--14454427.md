# Announcement on the Maintenance of ETH (Ethereum) and ETH Layer 2-related Network Wallet Deposit and Withdrawal Functions
**Dear Users,**

The ETH (Ethereum) team will conduct a hard fork upgrade on **December 3rd, 2025, at 21:50 (UTC)**. To accommodate this upgrade, Webot will suspend deposit and withdrawal services for ETH (Ethereum), Arbitrum (ARB), Optimism (OP), and Base (BASE)-related tokens on **December 3rd, 2025, at 19:00 (UTC)**. Services will automatically resume once maintenance is complete.

**Please note:**

- During wallet maintenance, users can still trade.

- Please deposit in advance. Webot will assist users with all technical issues involved.

- Webot will reopen deposits and withdrawals for related tokens once the network is stable after the hard fork upgrade.

Thank you for your support of Webot!

Webot Team
December 1st, 2025
