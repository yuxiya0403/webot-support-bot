# How does Webot use my ID?

Before gaining access to the buy and sell features, you will be asked to verify an ID with us. We go to great lengths to keep this information safe. Employee access is heavily restricted, and we require background checks on all employees.

Our primary purpose in collecting personal information is to provide you with a secure, efficient, customized experience. Additionally, verifying your identity helps prevent fraud on our platform. We will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties. 

For more information, please check out our [Privacy Policy](https://blog.webot.com/en/2026/01/12/webot-privacy-policy/) and [User Agreement](https://blog.webot.com/en/2025/12/29/webot-user-agreement/).
