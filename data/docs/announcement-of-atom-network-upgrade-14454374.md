# Announcement of ATOM Network Upgrade
Dear users,

Due to the upgrade of the **ATOM** network, Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal of **ATOM (Mainnet) at 09:30 on February 15th, 2023 (UTC)**. It will resume after the upgrade is completed. We apologize for any inconvenience caused during the upgrade.

**Risk warning:**

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
