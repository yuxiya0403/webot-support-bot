# How to Sign in via QR Code
Logging into your Webot account via QR codes is a secure and convenient method through your mobile app. Here's a quick guide:

**Step 1: **Open your Web browser and visit the [Webot sign-in page](https://accounts.webot.com/en/sign).

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2207546563/cef66a1db39b817befd4468acee5/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=fa14ca524d1b644262571ca120dc0a37305602f7821d72130b75c3cb4f0e1993&req=diInEcx6m4RZWvMW3nq%2BgW6qc8Gk1m49boJXlweXYNqULAv7eF8dqEuey%2FXB%0Azqh9NkhOc0ino5Kr8HYkMNYDddg%3D%0A)

**2. **Once on the sign-in page, your unique QR code will be automatically generated and displayed. This QR code is your key to quick and secure access.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2207494752/e4b707aa313594a482c6628b4c59/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=530c0e6356519e1bcb700c04e596c20e6bd623fb52a4a793aca356ad39d5bde1&req=diInEc13mYZaW%2FMW3nq%2BgWwqwPvV%2FfGxEMei%2FLvXcUeIa8e3WNZia0dnxVH8%0A15vzZRDsnkhMxd%2Fa4pqoQcOe%2BeY%3D%0A)

**Note:** The QR code in your web browser is valid for 2 minutes. If it expires, click [**Refresh**] for a new QR code

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2207536943/6e91bd9a09b689bb552ce66c40fb/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=2e1368ab3fe0d1b952b7bc12e575e0891c2c9e4be9e2155f009f832e15a245c9&req=diInEcx9m4hbWvMW3nq%2BgWEyPnxOUOCBf3YwjTd13Kxw7yALadTOg6R%2BfkOl%0ARyxnrDZ9BxvZZROzcy6iRWGjVQA%3D%0A)

**3. **Open the [Webot mobile app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download) and click the QR code scan icon, located in the top right corner of the app.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2207552509/0fa3524d3b15041c9361e967b1ff/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=14143066721fcd5f9de6eb9a669d233b50ee2161e93cef25dade016a3aa5f471&req=diInEcx7n4RfUPMW3nq%2BgST6wVgeb5uT0FEnGBJjXGHkztU1ENx8Fd%2FIbaEV%0AEykaMo9d8fgS2Z2g7oEbjeESEy8%3D%0A)

**Step 4:** Use your phone's camera to scan the QR code displayed on your web browser. After successfully scanning, tap **[Continue to sign in]** within the mobile app.

You can select **[Stay signed in for 14 days]**, which will allow you to remain logged in without needing to scan again for the next 14 days.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2207562385/e1a1d5f41b7c27cc43c6c058b99a/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=c9006ee65d7f76225b6766f12d2d1909fca6531b72d353316551d2e9e8c440a1&req=diInEcx4n4JXXPMW3nq%2BgQNIjSKUcd827UPOe5S8RqqfZZg29sU%2FSadWGq2I%0AMyXTXMqelVs0rKV4TpjOG8MvDmE%3D%0A)
