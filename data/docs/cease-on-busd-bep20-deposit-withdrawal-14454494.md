# Cease on BUSD(BEP20) Deposit & Withdrawal
Dear users,

Webot will cease the deposit and withdrawal services of BUSD(BEP20) **at 08:00 on September 6th, 2023 (UTC)**. Users can still use Ethereum (ERC20) of BUSD for deposit and withdrawal services.

Involving tokens:

**coin**

**chain**

BUSD

BNB Smart Chain(BEP20)

**Please notice:**

- Any related tokens deposits that are mentioned above will not be credited after the cease of BUSD(BEP20) deposits and withdrawals.

- As Paxos has [halted minting of new BUSD](https://paxos.com/2023/02/13/paxos-will-halt-minting-new-busd-tokens/), Webot will gradually cease support for BUSD-relevant services. Please convert BUSD-related assets into USDT. Relevant information will be announced separately.

- After the cease of BUSD(BEP20) deposits and withdrawals, users could still use other networks to deposit and withdraw.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
