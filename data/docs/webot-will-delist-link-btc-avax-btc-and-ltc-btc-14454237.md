# Webot will delist LINK/BTC, AVAX/BTC and LTC/BTC
Dear Users,

​

To enhance market liquidity and improve the overall trading experience, Webot will delist the following trading pair at 2:00 am UTC on May 26, 2025:

- LINK/BTC

- AVAX/BTC

- LTC/BTC

Please Note:

- 

All bot orders and manual orders associated with the above trading pair will be automatically canceled at the time of delisting.

- 

LINK, AVAX and LTC tokens tied to bot orders will be released to your trading account once those orders are canceled.

- 

Trading for LINK, AVAX and LTC will remain available via the LINK/USDT, LINK/USD, LINK/USDC, AVAX/USDT, AVAX/USD, LTC/USDT and LTC/USD pair, and users may continue to trade or create bots through these pairs.

Risk Warning

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
