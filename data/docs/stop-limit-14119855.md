What is Stop Limit

The Stop Limit bot means that the trigger price, the order price after the trigger, and the order quantity are set in advance. When the latest price reaches the trigger price, the bot will place the order at the preset order price.

For example, the current price of ETH is 2990 USDT, and 3000 USDT is the resistance level. You believe that if the price can break through the resistance level, the price will continue to rise, and you can buy more when the price reaches 3000 USDT. But you can't focus on the market for 24 hours. You can now use the Stop Limit bot to realize your ideas.

How to use Stop Limit

Please open [Webot](https://www.webot.com/en-US/) and log in to your account, then select the **“Stop Limit”** bot on the left side of the page.

![](https://static.intercomassets.com/assets/article_broken_image_placeholder.png)

After finding the “Stop Limit” bot, please click the **“Create” **button to enter the parameter setting page.

![](https://static.intercomassets.com/assets/article_broken_image_placeholder.png)

- **Trigger price: **When the “latest price” reaches the “trigger price” set by the user, the price will be triggered, and the order placed.

- **Buy/sell price:** After the trigger price is triggered, the order will be placed at the commission price.

- **Buy/sell quantity: **The number of orders placed after the trigger.

When the order is completed, the order is triggered, and the historical order of the Stop Limit can be viewed in the “history”. Unexecuted “Stop Limit” orders can be canceled at any time.

For Example

“Stop Limit(Sell)” Use Cases

Take ETH/USDT as an example: you buy 10 ETH at 3000 USDT, and the price is around 2950 USDT as the support level. If the price drops below the support level, it may continue to fall, and you need to stop the loss in time. Then you can order 10 ETH when the price reaches 2900 USDT.

“Stop Limit(Buy)” Use Cases

Take ETH/USDT as an example: the current price of ETH is 3000 USDT, and the ETH price near 3100 USDT is a resistance level through indicator analysis. If the price breaks through the resistance level, it may continue to rise. Then you can add an order of 10 ETH when the price rises to 3110 USDT.
