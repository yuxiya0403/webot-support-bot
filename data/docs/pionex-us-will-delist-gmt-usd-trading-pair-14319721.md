# Pionex.US Will Delist GMT/USD Trading Pair

Dear Users,

Due to the poor liquidity of** GMT/USD**, Pionex.US will delist the **GMT/USD** ** **trading pair** at 02:30 on November 8th, 2023 (UTC)**. Users can trade **GMT** with the **GMT/USDT **trading pair.

**Please Note**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Pionex.US will delist the **GMT/USD** trading pair and cancel the related manual orders and bot orders, and the **GMT **positions will be released to the trading account.

- Users can trade **GMT ** with the **GMT/USDT**** ** trading pair after delisting the **GMT/USD ** trading pair.
