# Webot Will Support the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) Network Upgrade & Hard Fork
Dear users,

[Webot](http://webot.com/) will support the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network upgrade and hard fork.

- [Webot](http://webot.com/) will suspend the deposit and withdrawal services of the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network at 03:50(UTC) on June 22nd, 2022, o support the network upgrade and hard distribution of BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) at the [BNB Smart Chain block height of 18,907,621.](https://bscscan.com/)

**Please Note：**

- The trading of tokens available on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network will not be affected during the network upgrade and hard fork.

- The BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network upgrade and hard fork will take place at the [BNB Smart Chain block height of 18,907,621](https://bscscan.com/). The estimated time is for users’ reference only.

- The BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network upgrade and hard fork will not result in new tokens being created.

- We will reopen deposits and withdrawals for the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network once we deem the upgraded network to be stable.
