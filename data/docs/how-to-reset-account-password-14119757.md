# How to Reset Account Password
You can reset your account password from the Webot web or app. 

**_Please note that for security reasons, withdrawals from your account will be suspended for 24 hours after the password reset._**

👉🏻 **[App operation step](#h_01KFJJHPFKESKGAR7R3AZS2030)**

👉🏻 **[Web operation step](#h_01KFJJHPFQZEYKJM7F43WF1NHP)**

**App operation step**

**Step 1:** Open the [Webot app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download), go to **[Account]** - **[Forgot password?]**. After reading the prompt, click **[Continue]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935717/2f557098f1d95bf3949523f38187/14961293927311?expires=1776232800&signature=dc818181f4f393ec090f5e107eca5fdbe470cf7092a57471a1f38e39dcfabb03&req=diEgEcB9mIZeXvMW3nq%2BgU0kUBa4siqlh56ovZfkDILSS1fItQlBVQau%2BhQb%0AScG6tZrFVd39BaGPOfEnUJFDW2E%3D%0A)

**Step 2:** Enter the email associated with your Webot account, and click **[Next step]**. Complete the CAPTCHA verification, then enter the Google Authenticator verification code and click **[Confirm]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935756/e0c7352868a2c244d94321a9fb9f/14961293927567?expires=1776232800&signature=fb9af28e8eb25b3b0492695f2adca782e35ca1c58e84b7aecc9083701a10d265&req=diEgEcB9mIZaX%2FMW3nq%2BgdxuBUSDyA0ADjoAyjNuUvHWa9lLR%2B2QjTKT1ulA%0AvIv8YcOQpmX8hAL8ett9eY813GA%3D%0A)

**Step 3**: Next, enter the email/SMS verification code and click **[Confirm]**. Enter your new password, re-enter the same password, and finally click **[Next step]** to complete the setup successfully.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935809/ff41a78cf4f7de13dfa2499c3d70/14961293927951?expires=1776232800&signature=31c1b2a3ec7d41b119aaa933cfb3582b979cf75de41b745a9b8f34c1ce043ec3&req=diEgEcB9mIlfUPMW3nq%2BgVkJHnuSro%2B3okl1jJKxX55eb96xHKIsYlNVEyz0%0AvKO9OKDPAMlAVBP5NOV5RccwRk0%3D%0A)

**Web operation step**

**Step 1:** Go to the [Webot website](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and click **[Sign in]** - **[Forgot password?]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935847/4eaaf858cca06329ef876d5f0bfe/14961283364751?expires=1776232800&signature=407d291a810363ccde4001d69a59d87fd62c90efd679942da54dad95c19442e9&req=diEgEcB9mIlbXvMW3nq%2BgcUubSCEVYH6QzXgSUtqIRAMb8gZFAvXKcZabX%2FZ%0AjQIxiZsRtDZ2ksYE2e8KTww%2B3y0%3D%0A)

**Step 2:** Enter the phone number or email associated with your Webot account, click **[Next],** and complete the CAPTCHA verification.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935888/219aa0656da4c576d9ede2edbe83/14961283365007?expires=1776232800&signature=26065bf04f1bf1940ac835b590b835c460f6ba962fbef30220964608bab65df6&req=diEgEcB9mIlXUfMW3nq%2BgfB05gBgCbEqfRORpGT0f4o9tDYv4a46o7LGVSDA%0ApNd%2BrKtMBJ%2Bsctyi9Loemxz0f5M%3D%0A)

**Step 3: **Enter the Google Authenticator verification code and click **[Confirm]**. Next, enter the email/SMS verification code and click **[Confirm]** again to continue.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935918/d22bd6d80661cb322ae90dbb1a1b/14961283365391?expires=1776232800&signature=689408aef1e42fa92521862dad0bb34d2a12a05eea279c0d4f6701dc0f5edcc8&req=diEgEcB9mIheUfMW3nq%2BgeOZhX8Kwgn1X8MH1%2FNUBhXlNDJiV8HktMfQPpTF%0AwCDR77VrRuHg1Phv8KmmTap5o2o%3D%0A)

**Step 4:** Enter your new password, re-enter the same password, and finally click **[Submit]** to complete the setup successfully.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177935932/2a39ef5a5f74ffb9c64216c9898f/14961293930383?expires=1776232800&signature=884aadb2ffb387d2794ad597a67b4c45fcb1bfed52a320276c0ebbf51a7ba71f&req=diEgEcB9mIhcW%2FMW3nq%2BgQjU2v%2FhgMgjOh2eTd71NTZXrVZDl6%2Ffo5noi9Iw%0AEBQJ72FPndpxoDl%2FOyHi6Rchk9M%3D%0A)

**FAQ**

**Q: I'm having trouble receiving Email/SMS verification codes. How can I resolve this?**

**A:** Please refer to the following appropriate guides to resolve the issues: [Can't Receive Email Verification Code](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119759-can-t-receive-email-verification-code), [Can't Receive SMS Verification Code](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119761-can-t-receive-sms-verification-code).

**Q: The system shows ‘invalid verification code’ when entering the email/SMS code. How to resolve?**

**A:** Please refer to the [Email/SMS Verification Codes Wrong](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119762-email-sms-verification-codes-wrong) article for troubleshooting.

**Q: What should I do if I get a Google Authenticator code error when I reset my password? **

**A:** Please refer to the [2FA Code Error article](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119751-how-to-solve-2fa-code-error) for troubleshooting.

**Q: What should I do if I lose Google Authenticator and can't reset my password? **

**A:** Please follow the instructions in the [Steps to Manually Reset Google Authenticator](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119764-steps-to-manually-reset-google-authenticator) article to reset your Google Authenticator first. Once the reset is successful, you can reset your password.

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
