# Webot Will Reopen BTC/USDC, ETH/USDC Trading Pairs
Dear users,

Due to the closure of BUSD automatic exchange channel, Webot suspended the spot trading of BTC/USDC and ETH/USDC. The spot trading of BTC/USDC and ETH/USDC will reopen on March 13, 2023 (UTC+8).

Please noted:

- Before BTC/USDC and ETH/USDC trading pairs reopen, all the bot and manual trade orders for BTC/USDC and ETH/USDC pairs will be canceled. Once trading reopened, the relevant trading pairs can trade.

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
