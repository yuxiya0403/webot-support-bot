# Keys to Success When Completing Identity Verification
Completing your identity verification (KYC) is a crucial step to securing your Webot account and accessing our full range of services. To ensure a smooth and successful verification process, please follow the guidelines and solutions below.

**Essential Document Requirements**

To avoid automated rejections, please ensure your government-issued ID meets the following criteria:

- **Validity:** All documentation must be current and not expired.

- **Physical Original:** You must upload a live photo of the physical document. Photocopies, digital screenshots, or scans will be automatically rejected.

- **Visibility:** The document must be fully visible from corner to corner. Ensure no parts of the ID are obscured, cropped, or covered by fingers/objects.

- **Clarity:** Photos must be high-resolution. Blurry images or those with significant glare/reflections will lead to rejection.

- **Supported Regions:** Webot currently supports specific U.S. states and territories. Please ensure you are a resident of a [Supported Region](https://blog.webot.com/en/2025/12/17/licenses/).

- **Accepted Types:** We currently accept valid U.S. State IDs, Driver’s Licenses, Passports, and Proof of Address (POA) documents.

**Face Verification (Biometrics) Tips**

During the "Selfie" or live-action face check, please keep these best practices in mind:

- **Lighting:** Ensure you are in a well-lit area with light facing you. Avoid backlighting (light sources behind you), as this obscures your facial features.

- **Background:** Do not use green screens, virtual backgrounds, or any filters.

- **Authentication:** The verification must be performed by the account owner. The presence of third parties in the frame or attempting to verify for someone else is a violation of our terms and may lead to permanent account closure.

**Proof of Address (POA)**

If you choose to verify your identity using a Passport, you are required to submit a supplemental Proof of Address (POA).

- **Accepted POA Documents:** Utility bills (water, electricity, gas), bank or credit card statements, tax invoices, or other government-issued residential certificates.

- **Note:** A valid U.S. Driver’s License may also serve as a Proof of Address.

- **Recency:** The document must be issued within the last **six (6) months**.

**FAQ**

**Q: My verification is still "Pending" after a few minutes. Is there a problem?** 
​**A:** Not necessarily. While many checks are instant, some applications require a manual system review. This process typically completes within 12 hours. We appreciate your patience.

**Q: Why was I rejected for "Duplicate Account"?** 
​**A:** Our policy allows only one verified identity per user. Please check if you have an existing account. If you recently closed a previous account, there is a **30-day cooling-off period** before your ID can be used to verify a new account.

**Q: Can I complete the verification on my computer?**
​**A:** Yes; however, we strongly recommend using the [Webot Mobile App](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download). Mobile cameras generally provide better focus for documents and more reliable biometric scanning than desktop webcams.

**Q: My application was rejected, but I don't see a specific reason.** 
​**A:** If the provided reason is unclear, please contact our 24/7 Online Support or submit a formal support ticket for a detailed review of your case.

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
