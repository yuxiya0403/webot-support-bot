**What is 3D Secure (3DS) ?**

3DS is a formal security verification that authorizes online payments by entering a password or a one-time PIN.

**How to complete 3DS verification?**

When the system asks for 3DS authentication, it will take you to a new web page where you will see one of two flows: a challenge flow where you will enter a PIN or one-time passcode, or a frictionless flow(Blank screen) where no explicit action is required (about 2-3 seconds), and the page will automatically jump back to [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/)when the authentication is complete.

**What are the reasons for expired or failed 3DS verification?**

- Your card issuer does not support 3DS.

- Your card issuer does not allow payments to crypto exchanges.

- You have not entered the 3DS verification code within 15 minutes or have entered an incorrect code.

**How can I solve the problem so that I can make a deposit?**

- Please check if you have entered the correct 3DS verification code within 15 minutes.

- Try to log in to the [Pionex.US](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/usdDW/debit/deposit) website operation for depositing.

- Contact the bank card issuer and ask them to lift the deposit restriction.

- Try using Pionex.US's other deposit methods (e.g., [Wire](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/usdDW/signature/deposit), [ACH](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/usdDW/ach/deposit)).
