# Webot Will Delist $MOBILE
Dear Users,
​

To enhance market liquidity and improve the overall trading experience, Webot will delist the following trading pair at 2:00 am UTC on June 29, 2025:

- MOBILE/USD

Please Note:

- All bot orders and manual orders associated with the above trading pair will be automatically canceled at the time of delisting.

- MOBILE tokens tied to bot orders will be released to your trading account once those orders are canceled.

- The MOBILE positions will be sold at the market price simultaneously when they are delisted, and the USD will be reissued to the users' holding accounts. If you are worried about price fluctuations, closing the bot in advance or selling them manually is recommended.
