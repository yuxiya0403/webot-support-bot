# Webot will delist FTT/USDT、FTT/BTC, and will list FTT/USD
Dear users,

Due to the affection by the upstream FTT spots delisting issue, Webot will delist FTT-related trading pairs **at 03:00 on November 15th, 2022 (UTC). **Webot will list FTT/USD trading pair, and users can still trade FTT tokens through FTT/USD trading pair after the FTT/USDT and FTT/BTC trading pairs are delisted.**
**
Spot trading pairs: FTT/USDT, FTT/BTC

**Please notice:**

- The FTT token assets will not be sold after the FTT/USDT and FTT/BTC are delisted. The FTT token assets will be retained in the Trade wallets after the corresponding bots are canceled.

- Webot will automatically close the trading bots' orders of FTT/USDT and FTT/BTC **at 03:00 on November 15th, 2022 (UTC)**. Users will not be able to create new orders. If you are worried about price fluctuations, it is recommended to manually close the orders in advance.

- During the FTT/USDT and FTT/BTC delisting period, the rebalancing bots orders with FTT balance will not be canceled. The holding position of FTT will be released to the Trade wallets.

- Users can still trade FTT tokens through FTT/USD trading pair after the FTT/USDT and FTT/BTC trading pairs are delisted.

**Risk warning：**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
