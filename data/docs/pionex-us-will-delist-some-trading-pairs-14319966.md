# Pionex.US Will Delist Some Trading Pairs
> Dear Users,
> To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the trading pairs related to the following tokens at 1...
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will delist the trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 12:00 on May 20th, 2024 (UTC):**

**CELO, DYDX, FTM, OMG, QTUM, REN, THETA, and TRX**

**Delisting trading pairs:**

CELO/USDT, DYDX/USDT, FTM/BTC, FTM/USD, FTM/USDT, OMG/USDT, QTUM/USDT, REN/USDT, THETA/BTC, THETA/ETH, THETA/USDT, TRX/ETH, TRX/USD, TRX/USDT

**Please note:**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th, 2022 (UTC).**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- Please cancel the bots, and manual orders and sell the above positions manually **before May 20th, 2024 at 12:00 (UTC)** for your rights.

- **CELO, DYDX, FTM, OMG, QTUM, REN, THETA, and TRX** positions in the bot orders will be released to the account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will not support the withdrawal features of the tokens mentioned above after delisting.

- If you can not sell or close the orders before delisting, we will automatically sell the delisted currency into USD or USDT.

**Risk warning:**

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will try its best to choose high-quality assets, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) can’t assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
