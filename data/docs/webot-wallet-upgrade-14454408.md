# Webot Wallet Upgrade
Dear Webot Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will be upgrading the wallets starting on **October 15, 2024, at 7:00 (UTC)**.

Please Note:
1. During the upgrade and maintenance period, the deposit and withdrawal functions of ALL TOKENS will be temporarily suspended.
2. This upgrade is expected to take approximately 1 hour. Once the upgrade is complete, deposits and withdrawals will be automatically reopened without further notice.
3. This upgrade does not involve any address updates.
