**Confuse Pionex.US and Pionex**

- **[Pionex.US](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/)** and **[Pionex](https://www.pionex.com/en-US/)** are two independent sites, and their account systems are entirely different. If you find the password wrong when you log in to your account, please check whether you have logged in to the wrong site.
​

**Incorrect password**

- Please check the caps of your password and whether you enter any extra spaces.

- If you forget your password, please click **“Forgot Password”** and reset it.

**The password is too long**

- Due to the system update, if your original password is longer than 16, please use **“Forgot Password”** to reset your password (8-16 digits).

**I can’t log in on the APP**

If your APP is not the latest version, it may display the account password error when logging in; please update your APP to the newest version.
