Dear users,

Pionex.US will support the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) upgrades and hard forks.

- Deposits and withdrawals on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) will be suspended starting from approximately 2022-10-12 03:00 (UTC).

**Please note:**

- The trading of BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) tokens will not be affected during the network upgrade.

- The BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network upgrades and hard forks will not generate any new tokens.

- During the upgrades and hard forks, deposits and withdrawals on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) will be suspended.

- We will reopen deposits and withdrawals for the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network once we deem the upgraded network to be stable.
