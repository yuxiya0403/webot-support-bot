# Announcement on Pionex.US Will Delist some Trading Pairs
> Dear Users,To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the following trading pairs at 08:00 on May 19th, 2023 ...
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the following trading pairs **at 08:00 on May 19th, 2023 (UTC)**:

- **XLM/BTC**

- **XTZ/ETH**

**Please note**

- This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Delisting trading pairs: **XLM/BTC、XTZ/ETH.**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- **XLM、XTZ **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Users can trade the assets mentioned above with USDT-relevant trading pairs.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously.
