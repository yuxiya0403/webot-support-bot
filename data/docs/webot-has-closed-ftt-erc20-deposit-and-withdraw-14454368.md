# Webot has closed FTT(ERC20) deposit and withdraw
Dear users,

Due to a large outflow of FTT(ERC20) from FTT(ERC20) deployment contracts, the intent of this outflow of FTT is unclear, and Webot is now closing FTT(ERC20) deposit and withdrawal.

**Related information**

[FTT deployment of smart contracts](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x048906570650e3c282e42832de167a0270c7890c77192362a0bff0f5bf415d3b)
[A large number of FTT outflow txid](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x87fa620c0302047bee0713f1c128dfa59d7fc4a3466f876be18fda3a3f19bf2c)

**Note**

- FTT (ERC20) depositing and withdrawing have been closed.

- FTT-related transactions will not be affected.

- If you deposit FTT (ERC20) before or after the announcement and it has not arrived, you can contact [Webot customer service](https://static.zdassets.com/web_widget/latest/liveChat.html?v=10#key=pionexus.zendesk.com) to request a token refund.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot can‘t assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
