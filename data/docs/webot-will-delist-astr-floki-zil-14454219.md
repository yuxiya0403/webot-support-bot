# Webot Will Delist ASTR、FLOKI、ZIL
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to the following tokens at 12:30 on March 28, 2025 (UTC): ASTR, FLOKI, and ZIL.

Please Note:

- Trading pairs scheduled for delisting: ASTR/USD, FLOKI/USD, and ZIL/USD

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- ASTR, FLOKI, and ZIL positions will be released to your trading account once the associated bot orders are canceled.

- Trading of these assets and creation of automated trading strategies will remain available through the respective USDT trading pairs (ASTR/USDT, FLOKI/USDT, ZIL/USDT).

Risk Warning

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
