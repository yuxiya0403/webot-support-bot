# ACH Deposit Maintenance
Dear Users,

We will be undergoing maintenance for our ACH Deposit function **from 19:00 EST on November 10th, 2023.**

All ACH Deposits made before the maintenance will be processed as normal. During the maintenance, the ACH Deposit function will be temporarily unavailable, and users will not be able to add new bank accounts. After the maintenance is completed, ACH deposit functions will be restored, and we will issue a new announcement. We apologize for any inconvenience during the maintenance.
