# BTC Wallet Upgrade and Address Change
Dear Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, [Webot](http://webot.com/) will begin upgrading the **BTC wallet on May 9th, 2024, at 07:00 (UTC). The expected upgrade time is 5 minutes.

**
Please note:

**1. This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th, 2022 (UTC).
****2. After this upgrade, users will receive a new BTC deposit address.
****3. It is recommended that users obtain the new address to deposit coins after the maintenance period.
**4. During the upgrade, deposit and withdrawal functions will operate normally and will not be affected. There will be no further notification upon completion of the upgrade.
5. For a while after the upgrade, deposits to the old address will still be credited until further notice.
