# Webot Will Delist BUSD-Relevant Trading Pairs
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the following trading pairs **at 08:00 on June 15th, 2023 (UTC)**:

- **BTC/BUSD**

- **ETH/BUSD**

- **BNB/BUSD**

- **BUSD/USDT**

**Please note**

- This announcement only affects registered users **before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Delisting trading pairs: **BTC/BUSD, ****ETH/BUSD, ****BNB/BUSD, ****BUSD/USDT****.**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- **BTC, ETH, BNB, and BUSD **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Users can trade the assets mentioned above with other relevant trading pairs.

- Webot supports the withdrawal feature with the **BUSD** tokens. Users can trade and withdraw **BUSD** tokens before the delisting time. The trade and withdrawal functions will be closed after delisting, and the positions of BUSD will be sold simultaneously at the market price upon delisting. The basic tokens will then be reissued to the users' holding accounts.

**Risk warning**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will do its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thanks for your support!
