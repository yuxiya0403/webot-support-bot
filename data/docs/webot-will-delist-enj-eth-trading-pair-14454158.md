# Webot will delist ENJ/ETH trading pair
Dear users,

Webot periodically reviews the liquidity of all our markets to ensure that the liquidity keeps meeting our high-level standard. When a token no longer meets the standard, we will consider delisting it.

Based on the bad liquidity of ENJ/ETH, we decided to delist the above trading pairs at 08:50 on August 11th, 2022(UTC), you can still trade the corresponding currency through other trading pairs.

Please note:

- All trade orders will be automatically removed after trading ceases in the respective trading pair.

- After trading is stopped, the trading robot will be automatically terminated.

Risk warning:

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will do its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
