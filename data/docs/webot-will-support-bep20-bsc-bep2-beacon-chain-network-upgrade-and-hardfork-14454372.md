# Webot Will Support BEP20(BSC), BEP2(Beacon Chain) Network Upgrade and Hardfork
Dear users,

Webot will support BEP20 (BSC) and BEP2 (Beacon Chain) network upgrades and hardfork, the specific arrangements are as follows:

- Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal of the BEP20(BSC) network at 09:00 on December 12, 2022 (UTC) to support the network upgrade and hardfork of BEP20(BSC) at **[block height 23,846,001 of the BSC network](https://www.bscscan.com/)**.

- Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal of the BEP2 (Beacon Chain) network at 06:00 on December 14, 2022 (UTC) to support the network upgrade and hardfork of BEP2(Beacon Chain) at [**block height****284,376,000 of the BEP2 network**](https://explorer.bnbchain.org/).

Please note:

- During network upgrades and hardfork, transactions of BEP20 (BSC) and BEP2 (Beacon Chain) network-related tokens will not be affected

- During the BEP20 (BSC) and BEP2 (Beacon Chain) network upgrades and hardfork, the deposit and withdrawal of related network tokens will be suspended

- During the period of network upgrade and hardfork, the deposits of relevant network tokens will not be credited to the account and will be automatically credited after the maintenance is completed. Please arrange the deposit and withdrawal in advance

- The BEP20 (BSC), BEP2 (Beacon Chain) network upgrade and hardfork will not generate new forked tokens

- After the network upgrade and hardfork, Webot will reopen the deposit and withdrawal services related to BEP20 (BSC) and BEP2 (Beacon Chain) after the network is stable, and no further announcement will be made

Details reference:

- **[BNB Smart Chain Mainnet Gibbs Upgrade Announcement](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/blog/bnb-smart-chain-mainnet-gibbs-upgrade-announcement/)**

- **[BNB Beacon Chain v0.10.3](https://github.com/bnb-chain/node/releases/tag/v0.10.3)**
