A string of letters and numbers that is used to access and spend cryptocurrencies or NFTs stored in a wallet. A Seed Phrase represents your private keys within a wallet.

Neither your private keys or seed phrase should ever be shared with anyone otherwise they could gain access to your wallet and steal your funds.
