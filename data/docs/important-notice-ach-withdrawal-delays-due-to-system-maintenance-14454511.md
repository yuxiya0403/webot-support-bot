# Important Notice: ACH Withdrawal Delays Due to System Maintenance
Dear Webot Users,

Due to system maintenance, withdrawals through ACH will take longer, and there is a possibility of failures. We recommend choosing Wire transfer for withdrawals.

For users who are already in the withdrawal process via ACH, please contact customer service for assistance if you need to switch to a different withdrawal method.

Our support team: [service@webot.com](mailto:service@webot.com)

Thank you for supporting Webot.
