# How to Connect & Deposit via your Bank Account (ACH)
This article will teach you how to connect your bank account via ACH and how to use ACH to deposit USD from your bank account to your Webot account.

_*Note: ACH Deposits can only be withdrawn in USD or Crypto after 14 days. Learn more [here](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868779244559-Why-is-a-Portion-of-my-Assets-Unavailable-Unable-to-be-Withdrawn). If necessary, please use a wire deposit to avoid such holds._

**Adding an ACH Bank Account**

**How to Connect an ACH Bank Account on the Mobile App**

1. Log in to your account, then click** [Home]** – **[Deposit]** – **[Bank Account]** –** [Link Your Bank** **Account]** – **[Confirm] - [Enter Amount]**.

**Troubleshooting & Other Important ACH Information**

**Withdrawal Lock Period**

Please be advised that ACH deposits (in USD or cryptocurrency) cannot be withdrawn until **14 days** have passed from the date the deposit was initiated. For additional information, please refer to the [article](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868779244559-Why-is-a-Portion-of-my-Assets-Unavailable-Unable-to-be-Withdrawn). You may consult your [deposit and withdrawal history](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/orders/deposit/ach) to determine when your deposit holds will expire. (If you do not see the “Withdrawable Time,” please note that it will be displayed once your ACH deposit has been fully settled.)

**How to increase the ACH deposit limit**

Webot will evaluate the following factors to determine if the ACH deposit limit can be increased.

- The length of time your account has been authenticated.

- The average amount of assets held in the account over time.

- The amount of assets currently held in your account.

**Unable to Find your Financial Institution**

If you are unable to locate your financial institution in the Stripe list (even after utilizing the search bar), please consider making a deposit through Wire Transfer or Debit Card initially.
