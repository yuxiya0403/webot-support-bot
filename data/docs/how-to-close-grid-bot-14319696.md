**👉🏻 [Web operation step](#h_01H51TYHA5JFBQTBPPKNHSNTC5)**

Web operation step

Open the [Pionex.US](https://pionex.us/en-US/trade/BTC_USD/Bot) webpage and log in to your account, then click **[Trade] - [Bot] - [Detail] - [Stop the Bot] **to close the bot.

App operation step

Open the app and log in to your account. Click **[Bot]**, find the bot order, and click **[Edit/Stop]** -** [Stop the bot]** to close the bot.
