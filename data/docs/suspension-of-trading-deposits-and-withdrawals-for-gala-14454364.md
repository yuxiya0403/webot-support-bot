# Suspension of Trading, Deposits and Withdrawals for GALA
Dear users,

Due to a cross-bridge misconfiguration with the multi-link routing protocol pNetwork, a BNB Chain address has minted over $1 billion pGALA tokens out of thin air. Webot suspended the trading and deposit and withdrawal services of GALA **at 23:00 on November 3rd, 2022 (UTC)**.

​

Please note:

- The affected token is GALA(BEP20), and Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal services of GALA(BEP20).

- During the trading suspension, running bot orders will not be canceled.

- Any GALA deposits will not be credited after the suspension of GALA deposits and withdrawals.

**Risk warning:**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
