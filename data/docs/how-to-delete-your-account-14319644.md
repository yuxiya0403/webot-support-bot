# How to delete your account

If you need to delete your account, please open your app and log in to your account, then follow the steps below.

**Step 1 Go to the delete account page**

Please click **"Account"** - **"Setting"** - **"Profile"** - **"Delete account"**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009385/8f8959cb545b1d4836d9053e7ca2/10685255898265?expires=1776232800&signature=15ea48ce8d8c34c62ac639807f0501681df818d039bc5f116f43b22a77825526&req=diImHsl%2BlIJXXPMW3nq%2BgVMvRNHJw1w97T4VLsC1Z9B%2FF6Gm3Mr9SSk1NT2b%0AZhno%2FvUbg%2F83FoCC%2FRWHHfa3pAs%3D%0A)

**Step 2 Confirmation of Notes**

Please read the notes and risk warnings for deleting the account, and then click **"Confirmation"** - **"Next step"** in order.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009411/53c25383b8becdbf9a0b0ab3d53a/10684977810585?expires=1776232800&signature=c841a67ccf893476c91aecac13df925f558288a273d27741c4dcc8f9da573b89&req=diImHsl%2BlIVeWPMW3nq%2BgR7QgGBymoFfHzT%2BWED9cq6JBS99HPMvOaEZB7mm%0A7wtl1qE7MX%2BuapbMAIzeY9Cn0eM%3D%0A)

**Step 3 Withdrawal of assets**

If you still have assets in your current account, following the instructions in step 2 and clicking on **"Next step"**, you will see the following page. Please follow the steps in the picture to process and withdraw your assets.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009439/d9d10f7893db03842c7c2babf899/10684982394649?expires=1776232800&signature=c7f339ad2fb5d5a68d1afca6dd14ec2ca0f47298eda48d029653b102557a65e7&req=diImHsl%2BlIVcUPMW3nq%2BgXbQuCQajvPpidnlVjmWzagVqmQ2nSS8YbQEPvGT%0Aib5TIlrIyRSfVEKzP%2BQzvkFHSKI%3D%0A)

**Step 4 Delete account**

If you have already withdrawn your assets, then after you click **"Next step"** as instructed in step 2, you will need to drag the slider to the right to complete the verification.

Then enter your email verification code to verify and click the **"Confirm"** button, and you will successfully delete your account.
