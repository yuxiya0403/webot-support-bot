# List of Unsupported States/Regions for Registration
We're sorry, but new user registration for Webot accounts is currently unavailable in the following US states and territories. Existing users are not affected and can continue to log in as usual.

- **Alaska**

- **Hawaii**

- **New York**

- **Guam**

- **Puerto Rico**

- **American Samoa**

If registration becomes available in your region in the future, we will notify you as soon as possible. For any other questions, please contact the Webot support team.
