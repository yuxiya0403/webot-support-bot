# Announcement on BTC Deposit Address Changed
Dear User,

Please note that the BTC network deposit addresses for BTC have changed. To protect your assets, please manually update all deposit addresses you have saved previously. Webot cannot change addresses you have stored elsewhere.

Please note that this announcement only affects registered users before December 28th, 2022 (UTC).**

**

Please be sure to check the addresses in the following scenarios:

(1) On-chain wallets, such as MetaMask, TrustWallet, etc.

(2) Whitelisted addresses of other exchanges

(3) Deposit addresses with paper backups

If you continue to use the old address to deposit, you may lose your assets and cannot recover them through Webot.

​

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your support and trust in Webot.

​

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact our customer service team at [service@webot.com](mailto:service@webot.com).
