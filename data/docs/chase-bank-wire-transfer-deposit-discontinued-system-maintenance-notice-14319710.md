Dear Pionex.US Users,

We sincerely inform you that in order to enhance system stability and user experience, we will be discontinuing the ability to make wire transfer deposits through Chase Bank on August 23, 2024 12:00 (UTC). The time of restoration is uncertain, please pay attention to our announcement notifications.

This means that after this date, you will no longer be able to top up your account through Chase Bank wire transfers. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

We recommend that you complete Chase Bank wire transfer deposits before this date. In the meantime, we have provided other safe and convenient deposit methods, such as Bank Account, Debit Card and wire via Lead Bank , for you to choose from.

If you have any questions during the process, please feel free to contact our customer service team, and we will do our best to assist you.

Service support email: [service@pionex.us](mailto:service@pionex.us)

Thank you for your understanding and support. We will continue to work hard to provide you with better services.
