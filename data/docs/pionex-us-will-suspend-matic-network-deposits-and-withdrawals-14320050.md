# Pionex.US will suspend MATIC network deposits and withdrawals
> Dear User,
> To provide better service, Pionex.US will upgrade and maintain the MATIC (Polygon) network deposit and withdrawal features at 0...
Dear User,

To provide better service, Pionex.US will upgrade and maintain the MATIC (Polygon) network deposit and withdrawal features **at ****08:00 on November 28, 2023 (UTC)**. During this period, all MATIC (Polygon) deposit and withdrawal functions will be closed.

Please Note:

- Users registered before December 28, 2022 (UTC) will not be affected.

- During the maintenance period, all MATIC (Polygon) network deposits will not be credited.

- Pionex.US will resume the MATIC (Polygon) network deposit and withdrawal features after the completion of the maintenance, and there will be no further notice.
