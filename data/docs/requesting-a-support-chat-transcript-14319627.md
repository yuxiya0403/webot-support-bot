- Find and click the

in the support chat window's bottom right corner. From the options, select "**Email transcript**."
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218012251/c5f0321f081899f3c27f1c3f6f4e/23148316681369?expires=1776232800&signature=76fd724276b0903e92a2bc0cb79f893c1cee563ffe900e8d40a145d94b1d2cdd&req=diImHsl%2Fn4NaWPMW3nq%2BgZLKXzy6QFk4Ykou86u0n%2FB56CwfwaBsDmHmBTWe%0ADhWE4GCBe2FJoAVDJJa1CWNZwEA%3D%0A)

- A window will appear; input your email address and click the "**Send**" button.

​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218012277/48d0ba0ece0a1a362f2b6aa829ea/23148308778393?expires=1776232800&signature=defaa2802e84648fd3d8ae6cb1a11a4e58a75325de873a8237422ace7df4662b&req=diImHsl%2Fn4NYXvMW3nq%2BgV0DH%2Fs7WCrYDyNs8TWoA6nfMQc0poVIQo4n0mNG%0A%2Bm%2BV%2BzLswT5dkH7l8PKZ4RT5dkY%3D%0A)

- You'll receive a notification, and the chat transcript will be sent to your email after your support chat concludes.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218012305/1505db2904afab5a64289f1a13ab/23148287626649?expires=1776232800&signature=35e9d2bd7878222b9da194b4443e7c167ac441884e78acd8b8868e1afd55e64a&req=diImHsl%2Fn4JfXPMW3nq%2BgYCo1idxHvc63qDkGyYHUk4VA1kNvb0tZ84KNHAj%0AZZ%2BM%2BkwB8lR%2BA47QxfICIsw%2FOlQ%3D%0A)
