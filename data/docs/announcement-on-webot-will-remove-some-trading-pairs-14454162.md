# Announcement on Webot will remove some trading pairs
Dear users:

In order to improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will remove the following trading pairs **at 12:00 on November 27th, 2022 (UTC)**:

- **SRM/USDT, SRM/BTC;**

- **TRIBE/USDT**

**Please note:**

- Webot will automatically close SRM-related and TRIBE-related trading bots' orders **at 12:00 on November 27th, 2022 (UTC)**. If you are worried about price fluctuations, it is recommended to manually close the orders in advance.

- The SRM and TRIBE token assets will be retained in the Trade wallets after the corresponding bots are canceled. The rebalancing bots orders with SRM and TRIBE balance will not be canceled. The holding position of SRM and TRIBE will be released to the Trade wallets.

- Webot will list SRM/BUSD and TRIBE/BUSD trading pairs as soon as possible after the SRM/USDT, SRM/BTC, and TRIBE/USDT trading pairs are delisted. Users can still trade SRM and TRIBE tokens through corresponding trading pairs.

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thanks for your support!
