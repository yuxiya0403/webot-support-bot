# Suspension on ADA (Cardano) Deposit & Withdrawal
Dear users, 

In order to support the ADA (Cardano) upcoming "Vasil" upgrade and hard fork, Webot has suspended the deposit and withdrawal of **ADA (Mainnet)**. After the upgrade is completed, it will be reopened. We apologize for any inconvenience during the upgrade. 

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
