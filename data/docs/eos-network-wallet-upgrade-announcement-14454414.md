# EOS Network Wallet Upgrade Announcement
Dear Webot Users,

To continuously optimize platform services and enhance user experience, Webot will upgrade the EOS network wallet on **March 28, 2025, at 08:00 (UTC)**. This upgrade is expected to take** 30**** minutes**, and the deposit services for the related network tokens will automatically resume once the upgrade is complete.

**Important Note:**
1. After the upgrade, the deposit addresses for the above-mentioned networks and tokens will be updated.
2. Users are advised to obtain the latest deposit addresses for deposits after the maintenance is completed.
3. During the upgrade, the deposit will be temporarily suspended and withdrawal functions will operate normally and will not be affected. There will be no further notification after the upgrade is completed.
4. After the upgrade, deposits made to old addresses will still be credited until further notice.
