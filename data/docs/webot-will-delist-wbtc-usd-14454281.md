# Webot Will Delist WBTC/USD
Dear Users,
​

To enhance market liquidity and improve the overall trading experience, Webot will delist the following trading pair at 4:00 am UTC on Jan 22, 2025:

- All bot orders and manual orders associated with the above trading pair will be automatically canceled at the time of delisting.

- WBTC tokens tied to bot orders will be released to your trading account once those orders are canceled. If you are worried about price fluctuations, closing the bot in advance or selling them manually is recommended.
