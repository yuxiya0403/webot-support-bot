# Webot Will Support ADA, SHIB, DOGE, and MATIC In the Buy Crypto Feature
Dear users,

Webot will support the following cryptos in the buy crypto feature on March 23rd, 2023(UTC).

- ADA

- SHIB

- DOGE

- MATIC

Webot has released the "[Buy Crypto](https://www.webot.com/en-US/buy-crypto/buy)" feature, allowing you to buy cryptocurrencies directly through your bank account (ACH) or Webot account balance. This feature provides a more convenient, faster, and secure way for users who want to buy cryptocurrencies.

**Advantages of buying cryptocurrencies on Webot**

For the "Buy Crypto" feature, Webot offers lower buy crypto prices than other regulated exchanges across the US, which means you can buy more cryptocurrencies on Webot for the same amount of USD.

_* According to the data from the third-party website: [buybitcoincompare](https://www.buybitcoincompare.com/?amount=1000)
