Web operation step

Open the [Pionex.US](https://v1.pionex.us/en-US/trade/BTC_USD/Bot) webpage and log in to your account, then click **[Trade]** - ** [Bot]** - **[Trading Bot]** - **[Detail]**
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218017134/7752df620682cc4986ce8b53fcd1/15605357580697?expires=1776232800&signature=7f3ded914e173cc297287b3ff69b68dbd92afb231be77f87dce2d3d9b828c7da&req=diImHsl%2FmoBcXfMW3nq%2BgQfr6QFUtkdDfCW1xdb0dA3nh4q%2BORbPefZ0nDHa%0AzaE4UMSUHNlInCs5a7aEVPSrkko%3D%0A)

Click **[More]** - **[Add investment]**, enter the amount of money you want to add, and click **[ok]**.
​

App operation step

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218017155/4a3e0512bca1b791e14b8905a300/15605334343321?expires=1776232800&signature=3482bb3c47a455978655c9f75418ba016aa2d975140e831a643dcf125aae73f2&req=diImHsl%2FmoBaXPMW3nq%2BgVkUvls08qroLcWCbl1K98YbBHPK%2FbAGUDCj9xYf%0A2FgusUBfoscpN%2F7%2Bhds32A8B7js%3D%0A)
