# Announcement on Handling Uncredited USDC Deposit
Dear users,

​

Webot has suspended the deposits of USDC from 11th March 2023 at 02:00(UTC). During the suspension period, USDC deposits will be refunded.

​

Please noted:

- USDC deposits will be reopened on 13th March 2023 at 12:00(UTC).

- Refunds for USDC deposits can be requested during the suspension period. Please contact customer service to apply.
