# Pionex.US 0% BTC Trading Fee Event Ends

Dear users,

**[Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/)**** 0% BTC trading fee event will end on Jul 25th, 2022 (UTC-4) from approximately 10 p.m. EDT (estimate only).**

[Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will automatically screen out users' orders with less than 0.1% profit per grid. If you are a non-VIP or non-MM user and might cause an order's profit loss with no 0 trading fee of BTC, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will provide some plans as follows for users to edit their order while closing the bots:

1. If the total profit of users' orders is positive, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will automatically close orders without selling your coins.
2. If the total profit of users' orders is negative, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will open MM qualification for those orders until users close their orders themselves or the order's total profit changes from negative to positive.
3. Users can lower the orders' grid quantity and ranges via the "Edit price range" function directly.
**4. BTC/USD will keep 0 trading fee, and other BTC-related trading pairs' trading fee will adjust from 0% to 0.05%. ETH-related trading pairs' trading fee will keep as 0.05%.

**Note:
1. When the total profit of users' orders change from negative to positive, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will notify users to close the bot or users can edit the orders' grid quantity and ranges. If users don't close the bots within the designated time, [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will close the bots for users and cancel MM qualification directly.
**2. Users can keep using the 1000 grids quantity of BTC grid trading bot, which empowers you to a higher frequency of scalping from the Grid Trading Bots.
