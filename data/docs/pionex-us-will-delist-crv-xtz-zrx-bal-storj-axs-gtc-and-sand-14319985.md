# Pionex.US Will Delist CRV, XTZ, ZRX, BAL, STORJ, AXS, GTC, and SAND
> Dear Users,To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to ...
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 08:00 on June 13th, 2023 (UTC)**:

**Tokens: CRV, XTZ, ZRX, BAL, STORJ, AXS, GTC, SAND**

**Please note**

- This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Delisting Trading Pairs:

- **CRV/BTC, CRV/USD, CRV/USDT**

- **XTZ/BTC, XTZ/ETH, XTZ/USDT**

- **ZRX/BTC,ZRX/ETH, ZRX/USDT**

- **BAL/BTC, BAL/USDT**

- **STORJ/BTC, STORJ/USDT**

- **AXS/USDT**

- **GTC/USDT**

- **SAND/USDT**

**
**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- **CRV, XTZ, ZRX, BAL, STORJ, AXS, GTC, and SAND **** **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Pionex.US will support the withdrawal feature with the above-mentioned tokens. Withdrawals or trades can be completed before the delisting time.

- For assets that have not been processed during delisting, Pionex will sell the assets at the best available price upon delisting and subsequently issue the corresponding USDT.
