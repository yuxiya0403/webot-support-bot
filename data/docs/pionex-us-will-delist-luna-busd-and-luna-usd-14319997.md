Dear users,

[Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) will support the rebranding of the Terra network to the Terra Classic network. LUNA tokens on the Terra Classic network will be referred to as LUNA(old) in the announcement. Further details are shown in the timeline below:

- At 14:50 on May 26th, 2022(UTC), trading will be stopped and the LUNA/BUSD and LUNA/USD spot trading pair will be delisted. The corresponding spot pending orders and robot orders will be canceled;

- The ticker for the LUNA (old) token will be renamed as LUNC.

**For the further plan of LUNC listing and other Terra-related programs, ****[Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/)**** will have another announcement to declare.
