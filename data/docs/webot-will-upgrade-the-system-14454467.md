# Webot will upgrade the system
Dear Webot Users,

[Webot](http://webot.com/)will soon upgrade our system to bring you better crypto trading experiences. All existing users will not be affected by the upgrade and will be migrated to the new system gradually. The announcement will be made soon for the details on the upgrade and migration.

First of all, the new system will bring you the Ethereum staking service. Meanwhile, we will delist some trading pairs which have very low daily volume. The existing system will continue to operate with a new web url [www.webot.com](http://www.webot.com/). The new system will operate with [www.webot.com](http://www.webot.com/). Please make sure you are accessing our service with the official [Webot](http://webot.com/)domain name.

We will continue to provide the best crypto trading bots in the industry and thank you for your trust in us.
