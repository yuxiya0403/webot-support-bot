# Announcement on Upgrading Render(RNDR) Token to Render(RENDER)
We will delist the RNDR/USD trading pair on August 13, 2024 at 06:00 (UTC+0).

Note:

Trading pairs to be removed: RNDR/USD.

After trading stops, trading bot orders and manual orders for related trading pairs will be canceled, but orders containing RNDR assets in the Rebalancing Bot will not be canceled, and the RNDR holdings in the Rebalancing Bot will be released.

Webot will support the RNDR replacement plan. The specific arrangements are as follows:

Webot will adopt Render(RENDER)as the token code for Render(RNDR).
All Render(RNDR) tokens will be replaced with Render(RENDER) tokens at a ratio of 1 Render(RNDR)= 1 Render(RENDER).
Webot will assist users who hold RNDR tokens in their Webot accounts to handle any technical issues involved.

Risk Warning:
Trading digital assets involve significant risks. Please purchase cautiously and be aware of the trading risks. Webot will select high-quality tokens but does not bear any guarantee or compensation responsibility for your trading behavior.

If you encounter any issues, please contact our customer service through the WEB or APP online customer service entrance in a timely manner. Thank you for your continuous support of Webot!
