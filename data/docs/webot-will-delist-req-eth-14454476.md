# Webot Will Delist REQ/ETH
Dear users,

​

Due to the lack of liquidity of the **REQ/ETH **trading pair, the transaction will cause high slippage. Webot will delist the mentioned trading pair **at 08:00 on February 8th, 2023(UTC)**.

​

Please note:

- At the same time as the above trading pair is delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pair will be canceled. The REQ positions will be released to the trading account.

- After the above-mentioned trading pair is delisted, users can still trade REQ with the **REQ/USDT **pair.

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.

Thanks for your support!
