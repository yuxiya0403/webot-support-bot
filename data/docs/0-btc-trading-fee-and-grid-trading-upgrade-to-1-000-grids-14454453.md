**
1. Trading fees**

The trading fees will be adjusted from 0.1% to 0% for BTC and 0.05% for ETH:

- 0% trading fee for BTC, including the following trading pairs: BTC/USD, BTC/USDT, BTC/USDC, BTC/BUSD, mBTC/USD, mBTC/USDT

**2.** **Grid Trading Mechanism Upgrade**

Based on the adjustment of trading fees, users could operate Moon Bot and Grid Trading Bot with more grids:

- Moon Bot will increase to 1,000 grids, including the following trading pairs: BTC/USD, BTC/USDT, ETH/USD, ETH/USDT

- The grid number for Grid Trading Bot increased from 200 grids to 1,000 grids but is limited to the following trading pairs: BTC/USD, BTC/USDT, BTC/USDC, BTC/BUSD, mBTC/USD, mBTC/USDT

The upgrade of grid trading mechanism and trading fee adjustment will be starting July 8th, 2022 from approximately 10 a.m. EDT (estimate only).
