# How to Disable Phone Number/Email
If you have lost access to your phone number or email and are unable to receive SMS or email verification codes, please follow the appropriate instructions to unbind your account.

👉🏻 [The account can be logged in, and the phone number/email can still receive verification codes normally.](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15030483142287-How-to-Disable-SMS-Email-Manually-When-Logged-In)

👉🏻 The account has a phone number, email, and Google Authenticator linked, but the phone number or email needs to be unbound.

👉🏻 [The account has only a phone number and email linked, and one of them has been lost.](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/34789315473693-How-to-Disable-SMS-Email-Without-2FA)

👉🏻 [The account has only a single linked phone number or email.](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/34789254300445-How-to-Disable-SMS-Email-Without-2FA-Single-Method-Linked)

Please get in touch with[Webot Support](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) if you have any other questions.
