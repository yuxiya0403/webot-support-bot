# Webot (formerly Webot) Wallet Upgrade Notice
Dear Users,

Webot (formerly Webot) will perform a full wallet maintenance and upgrade on **March 30, 2026, at 07:00 (UTC)**. To support this upgrade, all cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals on Webot (formerly Webot) will be temporarily suspended starting from **March 30, 2026, at 06:50 (UTC)**. The maintenance is expected to take approximately **2 hours**, after which deposit and withdrawal services will automatically resume.

**Please Note:**

- Users can continue to trade normally during the wallet maintenance.

- Any deposits made during the maintenance will not be credited automatically, they will be processed and credited gradually once the maintenance is completed.

- After the upgrade is completed and the network is stable, deposit and withdrawal services will be reopened without further notice.

**Risk Warning:**

There are considerable risks in digital currency transactions; please purchase carefully and pay attention to transaction risks. Webot (formerly Webot) will select high-quality currencies but will not be responsible for guarantee, compensation, etc., for any of your transactions.

If you encounter any problems, please get in touch with our customer service in time through the online customer service portal on the WEB or APP side. We appreciate your support of Webot (formerly Webot) all the time!
