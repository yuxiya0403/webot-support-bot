# Announcement on EOS (EOS) Token Swap and Rebranding to Vaulta (A)
Dear Users,**

**Webot will support the token swap and rebranding of **EOS (EOS)** to **Vaulta (A)**. Accordingly, the following adjustments will be made to EOS-related features:

**1. Suspension of Deposit and Withdrawal Services**
At 11:00 (UTC+0) on May 24, 2025, deposits and withdrawals for EOS will be suspended.

**2. Delisting of EOS Trading Pairs**

At 12:00 (UTC+0) on May 24, 2025, EOS/USD trading pairs will be discontinued and delisted. All open orders and related bot orders will be canceled.

**3. EOS Position Snapshot and Token Swap**
After the trading pairs are delisted, a snapshot of users holding EOS will be taken. Eligible users will receive Vaulta (A) tokens at a 1 EOS = 1 A ratio.

**4. Reopening of A Trading**
The trading of A will be reopened at a later date, which will be announced separately. Please stay tuned for further updates.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot cannot assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
