# Google Authenticator is working correctly; how to reset it?
> If your Google Authenticator is accessible and working correctly, you can reset it by following the steps below, and we will show you how ...
If your Google Authenticator is accessible and working correctly, you can reset it by following the steps below, and we will show you how to do it separately for Web and App.

You can follow these steps to Reset your Google Authenticator Pionex.US.

👉🏻 [**Web operation step**](#h_01H2Q6JGY7KKZGPB5YAX5DR5MX)

**👉🏻 [App operation step](#h_01H2Q6JZVDX7GTZ7B4C61FVX87)**

Web operation step

**Step 1. Go Reset the Google Authenticator page**
After logging in to your account, click on your **avatar**, then follow the instructions in the picture and click on **"Account Setting" **-** "UNBIND (Google Authenticator)" **-** "UNBIND"**

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011164/ee2eb984e48887cf7b6fd0933470/10282095101721?expires=1776232800&signature=f36649ef5c02a4091e626fbbbecae4d579fbf20bc07288fa7a38483e43f836b5&req=diImHsl%2FnIBZXfMW3nq%2BgcbCAvlWqi6H1ux2Gf2OPiae6Qkw2QV5CTzn44bh%0ApMrwEzM8yDELqYc7r2Qg0ed9FEE%3D%0A)

**Step 2. Authentication **
Enter your Google 2FA Code, and finally click **"UNBIND"** to reset your Google Authenticator.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011194/71558c4c6b7196c6d47733304c9c/10282205827609?expires=1776232800&signature=dfc733c3be3c79a852905ef8f7c4bde952a53e99c8646e73b417fc2fab607b20&req=diImHsl%2FnIBWXfMW3nq%2BgdgxfkBX4ve6Fo6bnULEjKXcravjXkeGsfPiJ%2Be2%0A1cXcKuqdQux%2Fjt7c287yZQ4p7Q4%3D%0A)

App operation step

**Step 1. Go Reset the Google Authenticator page
**After logging into your account, please click **"Account"**-**"Setting"**-**"Security"**-**"Multi-factor authentication"**-**"Google Authenticator"** in the order shown in the picture below.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011268/51a03c5e1e0771df9950b9b3d89a/10281133637017?expires=1776232800&signature=b2a2b54c8808de972fbfffb688e1fc8312627fb50e6988f2ee83b96f75ad3216&req=diImHsl%2FnINZUfMW3nq%2BgYeGCNWZFmxtwbddfCT0XgreOCHmCMu6q435R%2Bys%0ArsDFs%2BrM2Q9xz6w05y4bj9uFsLE%3D%0A)

**Step 2. Authentication**

Click **"Reset Google Authenticator"**-**"I have current 2FA"**-**"OK"**, then enter the Google verification Code.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011319/aaab5f6e45f84a689125ad2074ba/10281326877081?expires=1776232800&signature=171edc1dbed6c7facb0f7619800123f5daab503a46760703a2e1036e09ab3025&req=diImHsl%2FnIJeUPMW3nq%2BgRVdqHS%2F6c%2FHpDOy7zxiO0fHHpDimdble5wSzVQO%0AKxjJaaHqhA3MBqCW9eLl4E9W1sg%3D%0A)

**Step 3. Confirm Reset
**After entering the Google verification code, click the **"Confirm"** button and see the **"DISABLE"** prompt. Finally, you successfully reset Google Authenticator.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011370/c61765628dffce452e37c57bc5fe/10281455030553?expires=1776232800&signature=52e3378dcf5a4ae688a5f3226f77a3fe97be61e77a0f787db3fc7b3ea8a60439&req=diImHsl%2FnIJYWfMW3nq%2BgcK7fM4xxyLm73KuK8MPzOU736MZ1%2FGDH9b3iG3F%0AfhZzTurIuYe3UuuNParqEBZhPS4%3D%0A)
