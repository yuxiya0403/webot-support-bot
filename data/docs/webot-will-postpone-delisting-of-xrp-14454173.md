# Webot Will Postpone Delisting of XRP
Dear Users,

We regret to inform you that the scheduled delisting of XRP, initially planned for June 15th, 2023, will be postponed. The exact timing for the delisting will be announced in a separate announcement. We apologize for any inconvenience caused.

Please Note:

- This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th,2022 (UTC)**
