# Wallet Upgrade and Address Change
Dear Webot Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Pionex will upgrade the AVAXC, ERC20, POLYGON, OPTIMISM, and ETC network wallets starting from **08:00 (UTC) on August 1, 2024.** The estimated upgrade time is 10 minutes.

**Important Notes:**

1. After this upgrade, the deposit addresses for the aforementioned network mainnets and tokens will be updated.

2. Users are advised to obtain the latest deposit addresses after the maintenance period for depositing funds.

3. The deposit and withdrawal functions will operate normally during the upgrade and will not be affected. Completion of the upgrade will not be separately notified.

4. For a period of time in the future, deposits sent to the old addresses will still be credited until further notice.

**Risk warning:**

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Pionex will make its best efforts to choose high-quality assets, Pionex could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
