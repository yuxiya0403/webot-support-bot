# Announcement on the Maintenance of KASPA-related network Wallet Deposit and Withdrawal Functions
Dear Users,

The KASPA team will conduct a hard fork upgrade on May 5, 2025, at 15:00 (UTC). To accommodate this upgrade, Webot will suspend deposit and withdrawal services for KASPA-related tokens 90 minutes in advance, on May 5, 2025, at 13:30 (UTC). Services will automatically resume once maintenance is complete.

Please note:

- During wallet maintenance, users can still trade.

- Please deposit in advance. Webot will assist users with all technical issues involved.

- Webot will reopen deposits and withdrawals for related tokens once the network is stable after wallet maintenance.

Thank you for your support of Webot!

The Webot Team
May 5, 2025
