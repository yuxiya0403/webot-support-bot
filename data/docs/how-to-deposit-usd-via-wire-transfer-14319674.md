**Step 1**: Log in to the Website
Visit [**Pionex.US**](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/usdDW/wire/deposit) and log in to your account. Locate the "**Wallet**" option in the menu bar. Once you access the "Wallet," click the "**Deposit**" option.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013248/e2683284da79f4978e7c71d6b5b7/21051035636249?expires=1776232800&signature=a1fa1a7131c4dcb987b43447acce86db406e3e2d5ca3601a0e510a961fc1b6b9&req=diImHsl%2FnoNbUfMW3nq%2BgQA%2FpVzbvPp4mArmzcZ35kB0u1gMnCJuYzGUlosW%0Av%2BIfVrXZAOkBVfg08RRXgGmv1KI%3D%0A)

**Step 2**: Select "**USD**" Deposit
From the available deposit options, choose "**USD**."

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013274/ced811acba4da63c28c7123874fe/21051035644313?expires=1776232800&signature=b2e7c534f544346049628edb33d10192a7a115d13bc3d844cd9b7d38c7229ce0&req=diImHsl%2FnoNYXfMW3nq%2BgddW7RZorB6sdJ0MYnu%2FUdqyjLDGu3qaaUS5C6ot%0A7a41xf6SWtD7crmtCLVINPl23L8%3D%0A)

**Step 3**: Choose "**Wire** **Transfer**"
Select "**Wire** **Transfer**" as your preferred option and choose "**Deposit**".

Note: Before depositing, you need to complete the Identity Verification. If you have not verified your Identity, please click the “[**Verify**](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/account/kyc)” button and submit the information according to the prompt message. The bank transfer information can be seen after the verification is completed.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013288/4f3e54bd2e1868c13f26ab2db6dd/21051019294745?expires=1776232800&signature=5e3d9fade3d475de1182a755723c3bba59b22b22b82e76ad70acfd70a0298c41&req=diImHsl%2FnoNXUfMW3nq%2Bgbyzv6FVYF%2BEElJ06FeDIWzFpaUQrYAnXIr%2Bv9e6%0ATqWAtj2uKv68PLLhVQGaYFebHME%3D%0A)

Please carefully read the Important Notes at the top. They are the most important things you should know before sending a wire.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013303/93c4f3f05df3cef4c4daf5d9cfc5/21050000707097?expires=1776232800&signature=294145f44419ef6e20c21fbf32e9078718dd2747f7cfdc0f77c5e4406ae40243&req=diImHsl%2FnoJfWvMW3nq%2BgcNnel1uhWaxs45X%2BcDhJSUuUgXVSnroJ3S2cQdH%0Asi2k7Ig0RuwG819DzJUgCtC3gl0%3D%0A)

Review the wire instructions. Please include your Unique reference code in the memo/notes/reference field when sending your wire. Different banks have different ways to send a wire, so please contact your bank directly for instructions on completing the deposit. Once you have submitted all the information needed for your wire, you can click “**I have completed the wire**”.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013318/08ffe70d40829d0e6fad5e878c82/9771277805465?expires=1776232800&signature=cdb9b3275f7142de0e8a2f147d667f77704b38e15b8e9532a4dac966aad1d581&req=diImHsl%2FnoJeUfMW3nq%2BgSUOy%2FcyF8N%2Ba6eLZtRTQLs0Y5hLTTRtSp%2FJU2WD%0AsqCa9adB%2FcLb%2BINcMbzZ4i8K4Z4%3D%0A)

NOTES:

- **Pionex is not responsible for delays** in wire transfers due to your bank or intermediary banks that your bank uses. You will receive the wire within 1-5 business days in general.

- **Pionex does not charge fees on wire deposits,** and we will credit your account according to the actual amount received. Any other fees you may incur from transferring funds into your account are charged by your sending or intermediary bank. We recommend reaching out to your bank prior to making a transfer to clarify any fees you may incur.

- **Pionex is not responsible for any impact this has on your bank account.** You should be aware that many banks are uncomfortable sending cryptocurrency-related wire transfers.

- **Pionex can only process wire transfers on weekdays.** USD Wire transfers can take up to one week to process. In rare cases, it can take longer.

- Pionex can only wire fiat to bank accounts in the name of the Pionex.US account owner. We can only accept wires from accounts in the name of the Pionex.US account owner.

- **Pionex can’t accept funds from** Family funds, Wholly owned entities, and Joint accounts, so please confirm your bank account type before sending a wire.

- You must include the correct memo(**Unique reference code**) in your USD wire transfers, or they will not be credited. The account identifier is unique, and it will not change.
