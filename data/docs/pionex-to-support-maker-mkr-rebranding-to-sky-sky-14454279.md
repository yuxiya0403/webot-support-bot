# Pionex to support Maker (MKR) Rebranding to Sky(SKY)
Dear Users,

[Webot](http://webot.com/) will support the rebranding of the Maker(MKR) into Sky (SKY).

**The specific arrangements are as follows:**

- Webot stopped trading and delisted MKR at **2:00 AM (UTC) on Sep 8, 2025**, all pending orders will be canceled, and robot orders of related trading pairs will also be canceled;

- Users may refer to the [announcement](https://forum.sky.money/t/sky-has-arrived/24959) from the project team for more information.

**Delist trading pairs**
MKR/USD, MKR/USDT

**Merge Details**

- MKR tokens will be converted to SKY tokens at the following ratios (listing times for relevant trading pairs are to be determined): 1 MKR = 24000 SKY;

**Risk Warning:**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though [Webot](http://webot.com/) will try its best to choose high-quality assets, [Webot](http://webot.com/) cannot assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
