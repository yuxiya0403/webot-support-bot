Crypto Deposit History

To view the Crypto Deposit History on your mobile app and website, follow these steps:

👉🏻 **[On the Mobile App](#h_01H5HPX0V1Z4G94PJB95XZFDBJ)**

**👉🏻 ****[On the Website](#h_01H5HQ4WG5SY6DKCZ1PSQPHJHH)**

On the Mobile App

1. Click **[Home]** to go back to the home screen.

2. Click on **[Deposit]**.

3. Click on **[History]**.

4. Select **[Crypto]** to check your Crypto deposit history.

On the Website

1. Please sign in to the [Pionex.US](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/balance/deposit) website, and click on** [Wallet]** --> **[Deposit].**

2. Click on **[Crypto]** under the **[Deposit]** tab on the left side of the screen.

3. Check your Crypto deposit history under the Deposit history tab.

Crypto Withdrawal History

To view the Crypto Withdrawal History on your mobile app and website, please follow these steps:

👉🏻 **[On the Mobile App](#h_01H5HQ5FJPF55MMBYMN3P79EV8)**

**👉🏻 ****[On the Website](#h_01H5HQ5RW9RYQCJKHHD0922VJ2)**

On the Mobile App

1. Click **[Home]** to go back to the home screen.

2. Click on** [Withdraw]**.

3. Click on **[History]**.

4. Select **[Crypto]** to check your Crypto withdrawal history.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218013206/b6a6d23f4736019adae7ae22c807/21280174635289?expires=1776232800&signature=e88bef11c1c0e798d7355dfa0c8f7bd639aa72b9cf6abd54ad3763cf68ac9721&req=diImHsl%2FnoNfX%2FMW3nq%2BgdjhBkxP6bLSX8B%2FdDxWfsfmsoZgp%2FUUuP92Iz3E%0AwccA9a%2BHnSmKovxjO103dq6UNYQ%3D%0A)

On the Website

1. Please sign in the [Pionex.US](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/balance/withdraw) website, and click on **[Wallet]** -->** [Withdraw]**

2. Click on **[Crypto]** under the **[Withdraw]** tab on the left side of the screen.

3. Check your Crypto withdrawal history under the Withdrawal history tab.
