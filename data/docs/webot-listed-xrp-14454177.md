Dear Users,

​

We are delighted to announce that XRP is now listed on Webot with the trading pairs:

- [XRP/USDT](https://www.webot.com/en-US/trade/XRP_USDT/Manual)

- [XRP/USD](https://www.webot.com/en-US/trade/XRP_USD/Manual)

**Notice**

- Deposits & withdrawals are now open for XRP.

- This announcement only affects registered users after December 28th,2022 (UTC).

**What is XRP? **

XRP is a digital asset that operates on a decentralized platform known as the XRP Ledger. Developed by Ripple, XRP aims to facilitate fast and low-friction value transfers, providing institutions with a reliable solution for cross-border transactions.
