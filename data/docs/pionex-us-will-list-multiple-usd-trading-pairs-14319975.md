Dear Users,

Pionex.US will list **RPL(Rocket Pool), ARB(Arbitrum), BLUR(BLUR), APT(Aptos), LDO(Lido DAO) on August 3rd, 2023 (UTC), **and open the **RPL/USD, ARB/USD, BLUR/USD, APT/USD, LDO/USD** trading pair.
**
Please Note****
**

- This announcement only affects registered users **after December 28th,2022 (UTC).
**

**More Details:**

- **What is RPL(Rocket Pool)?
**Rocket Pool is an Ethereum 2.0 staking pool. The protocol seeks to lower both the capital and hardware requirements for staking on ETH 2.0, adding to the decentralization and security of Ethereum.

- **What is ARB(Arbitrum)?
**Arbitrum is a suite of Layer-2 scaling solutions for Ethereum, with two live and operational Layer-2 blockchains, Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova. 

​

- **What is BLUR(BLUR)?
**Blur is a non-fungible token (NFT) marketplace designed for professional traders' needs by emphasizing speed and lower gas fees. The platform also addresses the issue of royalties by allowing all traders to customize the royalties charged on each listing.
**
**

- **What is APT(Aptos)?
**The Aptos Network is a Proof-of-Stake (PoS) smart contract platform that uses the Move programming language for virtual machine (VM) transaction execution. The consensus mechanism on Aptos is Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT), which means the network functions properly if up to less than one-third of the validators act dishonestly.

​

- **What is LDO(Lido DAO)?
**Lido is a liquid staking solution for Ethereum and other proof of stake chains. This allows users to stake their tokens without having to lock assets or maintain staking infrastructure.
