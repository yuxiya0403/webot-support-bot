# DOGE Wallet Upgrade and Address Change
Dear Webot Users,

To provide a safer and faster user experience, Webot will upgrade the DOGE wallet starting on **July 25, 2024, at 08:00 (UTC)**. The estimated upgrade time is 5 minutes.

**Notes:
**1. After this upgrade, users will receive a new DOGE (mainnet) deposit address.
2. It is recommended that users obtain the latest deposit address after the maintenance period to make deposits.
3. During the upgrade, deposit and withdrawal functions will operate normally and will not be affected. There will be no additional notification upon completion of the upgrade.
4. For a period of time after the upgrade, deposits made to the old address will still be credited until further notice.
