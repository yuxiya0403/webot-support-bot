# How can I check the status of my wire transfer deposit order?
> Before we receive your funds
> Your funds will appear in your account 1-5 business days after you send them. However, in rare cases, such as...
**Before we receive your funds**

Your funds will appear in your account 1-5 business days after you send them. However, in rare cases, such as during holidays, the time may be longer and [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/) cannot control it, because it depends on your bank.

**After we receive your funds**

You can check your transaction in the History:

- Sign in to [Pionex.US](http://pionex.us/)

- Select **Deposit-> History->USD->Wire** (from Application)

- Select **Deposit-> Wire transfer->History** (from Web)

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us via [service@pionex.us](mailto:service@pionex.us).
