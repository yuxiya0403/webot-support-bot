# Webot Will Delist Some Trading Pairs
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Webot will delist the trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 08:00 on April 27th, 2023 (UTC)**:

- **AMP,ONT,BAT,CHZ,COTI,ENJ,FARM,KP3R,ORN,POLS,POWR,SKL,WBTC,EGLD,IOTX,GLM,
LUNA
**

**Please note**

- This announcement only affects registered users before **December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Delisting trading pairs: 
**AMP/USDT,AMP/USD,ONT/USDT,ONT/BTC,BAT/USDT,CHZ/USDT,COTI/USDT,ENJ/USDT,
FARM/USDT,KP3R/BUSD,ORN/USDT,POLS/USDT,POWR/USDT,SKL/USDT,WBTC/BTC,
EGLD/USDT,EGLD/USD,EGLD/BTC,IOTX/USDT,GLM/BUSD,LUNA/USDT,LUNA/USD.
**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- **AMP,ONT,BAT,CHZ,COTI,ENJ,FARM,KP3R,ORN,POLS,POWR,SKL,WBTC,EGLD,IOTX,GLM,and LUNA **** **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Webot will support the withdrawal features of the tokens mentioned above after delisting.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
