# Announcement on SXP (Swipe) Mainnet Swap & Rebranding Plan to SXP (Solar)
Dear Users,

Webot will support the SXP (Swipe) mainnet swap and rebranding plan to SXP (Solar).

Further details are shown below:

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- All the SXP (Swipe) tokens will be migrated to the Solar Mainnet at a ratio of 
**1 SXP (Swipe) = 1 SXP (Solar).**

- Webot will suspend SXP(ERC20) deposit and withdrawal service** at 12:00 on May 12th, 2023 (UTC)**. After the upgrade is completed, the deposit and withdrawal service will be opened as soon as possible.

- After the suspension of SXP (ERC20) deposit and withdrawal services, SXP (ERC20) deposits will not be credited and may not be recoverable.

- The SXP Spot Trading will not be affected.
