**How to Buy Crypto**

**Mobile App:**

1. On the homepage, click **[Buy Crypto]** and select the cryptocurrency you wish to purchase.
​

2. After selecting the cryptocurrency, enter the amount you wish to purchase. 
​

You can click the unit on the right to choose whether to use USD or the token as the reference: if USD is selected, the system will calculate the estimated token quantity; if the token is selected, it will calculate the required USD payment.
​

3. Please click below to select your payment method.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2178141164/e0dcea36f9efc83b142cc29485ea/2.png?expires=1776232800&signature=54b9c2ecb7f1ef9c744b5d69005176214b93082e64d9d220ed25cca372941e39&req=diEgHsh6nIBZXfMW3nq%2BgZ24P3gInWhQrzuqq5Jr4Zril%2FIQCEH%2BmHghYTTO%0Abizq03tPie88KKtoOZ50TI8QRvU%3D%0A)

4. Once all details are confirmed, click **[Buy]** — **[Confirm]** to proceed.

_Note: If your preferred cryptocurrency is not listed in this menu but is supported by Webot, kindly utilise the [manual trading page](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/trade/BTC_USDT/Manual)._

**How to Sell Crypto**

**Mobile App:**

1. On the homepage, click **[Buy Crypto]** and select the cryptocurrency you wish to sell.

2. After selecting the cryptocurrency, switch to the Sell page and enter the amount you wish to sell.

You can click the unit on the right to choose whether to use USD or the token as the reference: if USD is selected, the system will calculate the estimated token quantity to be sold; if the token is selected, it will calculate the estimated USD amount you will receive.

3. Once all details are confirmed, click **[Sell]** to proceed.

_Note: If your preferred cryptocurrency is not listed in this menu but is supported by Webot, kindly utilise the [manual trading page](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/trade/BTC_USDT/Manual)._

**FAQ**

**Q: Why am I unable to convert the full amount of my tokens?**
​**A:** The system has a slippage control mechanism to prevent potential losses from market fluctuations. Therefore, it adjusts the maximum convertible amount based on the current token price.

**Q: Why is my purchase via Bank Account not credited immediately?**

**A:** Buying with a Bank Account works like an ACH deposit. The system will execute the purchase once the funds are received, so it follows the standard ACH processing timeline.
