# What are the Trading Fees on Webot?
> Please view Webot Fees for Trading Fees.
> For all trading transactions, both maker and taker fees are 0.1%.
> Notes: Webot reserves the right...
Please refer to the [Webot Fees](https://www.webot.com/en-US/fees) page for detailed information regarding the trading fees applicable on the platform.

For every trading transaction conducted on Webot, the fees are structured as follows: the maker fee is set at 0.1%, which applies when you add liquidity to the order book by placing limit orders. On the other hand, the taker fee is 0.5%, which is charged when you remove liquidity by executing orders that match existing ones on the order book.

Please note that Webot reserves the right to modify or update the trading fees at any time. Users are encouraged to regularly review the fee schedule to stay informed about any changes that may affect their trading costs.
