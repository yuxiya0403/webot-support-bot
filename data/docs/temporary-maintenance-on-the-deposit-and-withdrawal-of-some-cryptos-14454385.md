# Temporary Maintenance on the deposit and withdrawal of some cryptos
Dear User,

Due to network congestion, Webot will have short-term maintenance on the deposit and withdrawal of some cryptos, and the estimated time is 30 minutes.

**Please note:**

- The cryptos that have not been recharged cannot obtain a new address.

- Withdrawal function will not be affected.

Risk warning:
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will do its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot could not assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
