# Pionex to support Omni Network (OMNI) Rebranding to Nomina (NOM)
Dear Users,

[Webot](http://webot.com/) will support the rebranding of Network (OMNI) to Nomina (NOM).

**The specific arrangements are as follows:**

- Webot will stop trading and delist OMNI at **2:00 AM (UTC) on Sep 24, 2025**, all pending orders will be canceled, and robot orders of related trading pairs will also be canceled.

- Users may refer to the [announcement](https://www.nomina.io/blog/omni-is-rebranding) from the project team for more information.

**Delist trading pairs**
OMNI/USD

**Merge Details**

- OMNI tokens will be converted to NOM tokens at the following ratios (listing times for relevant trading pairs are to be determined): 1 OMNI = 75 NOM;

**Risk Warning:**
Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though [Webot](http://webot.com/) will try its best to choose high-quality assets, [Webot](http://webot.com/) cannot assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
