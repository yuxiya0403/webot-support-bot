# Pionex Will Support OCEAN Conversion to FET
In line with the merge of **Ocean Protocol (OCEAN)** into** Artificial Superintelligence Alliance (FET)**, Pionex will be supporting the token conversion process.

**The specific arrangements are as follows:**

- Pionex will stop trading and delist the OCEAN spot trading pair at 02:00 (UTC) on Aug 22, 2025, all pending orders will be canceled, and robot orders of related trading pairs will also be canceled.

**Delist trading pairs**

- Spot: OCEAN/USD

**Merge Details**

- All OCEAN tokens will be converted to FET tokens at the following ratios by 02:00 (UTC) Aug 29, 2025:
1 OCEAN = 0.433226 FET

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously. Though Webot will try its best to choose high-quality assets, Webot can't assure any profit and will not be responsible for your trading loss.
