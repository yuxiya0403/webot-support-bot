# How to Find Your Referral Invitation Link
> A step-by-step guide to finding your referral invitation link on Webot, for both the mobile app and website.
Webot's Referral Program lets you earn 20% commission rebates every time a friend you invited makes a trade. This guide shows you exactly where to find your personal invitation link and code — whether you're using the mobile app or the website.
​
​**Mobile App**
​
Tap **Account** → **Referral icon** → **Copy** next to your invitation code or link, then share it with your friends.

​**Website**
​
The fastest way is to go directly to the Referral Program page (make sure you're logged in first):  [https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/referral_program](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/referral_program)
​

Alternatively, click your **Profile avatar → Referral Program**, then click the copy icon next to your **My Invitation Link** or **My referral code**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2221216315/a7845f3f64327b4b9ca64d4a15e6/Group+1312320209+%281%29.png?expires=1776232800&signature=9669fcc36d8263bf9ab112eb66bc557ea6233ff8bf56dbc3e5b8486a77224989&req=diIlF8t%2Fm4JeXPMW3nq%2BgU1abyI4ZpZW%2F7H6cikQDYdXetS47Kw5bG8VHkLm%0Aq%2FBPsd4eAg7pwd4FA1xnh05K8vE%3D%0A)

_Note: Commission rebates are automatically credited to your account hourly in USDT. There is no need to manually redeem these funds, as they are deposited directly into your balance._
