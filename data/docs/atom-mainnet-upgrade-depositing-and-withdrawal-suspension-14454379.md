# ATOM Mainnet Upgrade: Depositing and Withdrawal Suspension
Dear users,

Webot will suspend the deposit and withdrawal services related to ATOM(Cosmos) at 06:30 on March 15th, 2023 (UTC) to support ATOM(Cosmos)at [Cosmos block height 14,470,501](https://www.mintscan.io/cosmos/blocks/14470501)— expected to upgrade network at 07:55 on March 15th, 2023 (UTC).

**Please noted:**

- During the ATOM(Cosmos) network upgrade period, FIL-related transactions will not be affected;

- Please handle ATOM(Cosmos)-related deposits and withdrawals in advance. During the deposit and withdrawal maintenance period, ATOM(Cosmos)deposits will not be credited;

- As the ATOM(Cosmos) network upgrade is complete, Webot will reopen the deposit and withdrawal service of ATOM(Cosmos) when the network is stable;

**Details:**

- [Cosmos v9-Lambda](https://www.mintscan.io/cosmos/proposals/187)

- [Cosmos Lambda Upgrade Countdown](https://www.mintscan.io/cosmos/blocks/14470501)
