Mobile App operation step

Please click **[Home] - [Deposit] - [History] - [Card] **to check the **[****Withdrawable Time]**.
​

Website operation step

1. After logging in to the account, click **[Wallet]** - **[Overview]**.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218017576/395a450aa8590ea4a67dcd075537/19722268149529?expires=1776232800&signature=0e9925ecda31035c729de07a9bfda4375e487b87de44fbef2e24c6781b66e118&req=diImHsl%2FmoRYX%2FMW3nq%2BgTtM3a1R2Jkt7Hi%2FQF1ORBoKzBCvl1%2Fqxi35pAQ6%0ANuU4qjOlUXUvrxy7J6XW7LX7vHQ%3D%0A)

2. Click **[USD]** under the **"Deposit"** tab.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218017597/1a1f083212da83d1e02fbdf52800/19722268152345?expires=1776232800&signature=d9b6ec4ce19b4f77a5db108bc8287df679b7aa52643c4bac577f1a0b91f7adac&req=diImHsl%2FmoRWXvMW3nq%2BgbQUoHC7%2FPTCDT4CielIFGeTNwFygQEGnuqA6w8g%0ABUoXt3v0Ibs9sZOvQJk6JNyCqJg%3D%0A)

3. In the **"Debit Card"** tab, click **[Order History]** - **[Withdrawable Time]**.
