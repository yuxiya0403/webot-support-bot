Why isn't my document being accepted?

Our verification provider may be unable to process your request for a few reasons. Here are a few tips to help complete this step.

- Ensure that your document is valid. We are unable to accept an upload of an expired ID or an ID that is expiring in 1 month.

- Ensure your identification document is in a well-lit area without much glare.

- Photograph the entire document, and avoid cutting off any corners or sides.

- A high degree of wear and tear on an ID can also get the ID to be rejected.

- Please avoid uploading edited photos of your ID, including scan copies, screenshot copies, cut copies. etc

- If you are having trouble with the camera on a desktop or laptop computer, try installing our [iOS or Android app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download) on your cell phone. Using your phone's camera, you can use the mobile app to complete the ID verification step. Open the 'Identity Verification' section by going to Account --> Setting --> Security --> Identity Verification.
