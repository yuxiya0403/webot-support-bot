# Comprehensive Guide: Upgrading from Pionex.US to Webot
1. Why is Pionex.US upgrading to Webot?

- **Brand Evolution**: Webot is the official brand upgrade of Pionex.US, meticulously designed to provide localized services that meet the specific needs of our US users.

- **Next-Gen Performance**: Built on a native architecture, Webot delivers a significantly smoother and more responsive trading experience.

2. Do I need to re-register or manually transfer my assets?

- **Seamless Login**: No new registration is required. You can log in to Webot directly using your existing Pionex.US email and password.

- **Automatic Sync**: Your funds, positions, trading history, and referral codes have been automatically synced 1:1.

- **Asset Security**: All assets are backed by a 1:1 reserve, ensuring your funds are secure and available for withdrawal at any time.

3. What new features does Webot support?

- **2025 Tax Reporting**: Official IRS-standard tax reports for the 2025 fiscal year will be available exclusively on Webot.

- **SSN/TIN Updates**: The ability to update your SSN/TIN will be provided exclusively on the Webot platform.

- **Future Innovation**: Moving forward, all new feature releases and system updates will be conducted solely on Webot.

4. Is Webot trustworthy?

- **48-State Compliance**: Webot is an officially certified Money Services Business (MSB) with NMLS ID: 2284360, operating under strict multi-state regulatory oversight.

5. Why are Candlestick Charts and Limit Orders missing on the Webot App?

- **Phased Rollout**: Webot is currently in a phased release cycle. **Candlestick charts and Limit Orders** are expected to launch in an upcoming update around January 15, 2026.

- **Dual-App Strategy**: If you require these specific features for immediate trading, we recommend using both the Pionex.US and Webot apps side-by-side for now. You can monitor markets on the legacy app while utilizing the latest tax and AI tools on Webot.

6. Is there a difference between the Web version of Webot and Pionex.US?

- **Functional Parity**: Currently, there are no functional differences between the Webot and Pionex.US web platforms.

- **Web Roadmap**: All future web-based updates and feature enhancements will be implemented exclusively on the Webot domain.

7. How do I start the migration?

- **Download the App**: Search for “**Webot**” in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

- **Direct Login**: Use your original Pionex.US account credentials to sign in.

- **Start Trading**: Experience the next generation of intelligent trading.
