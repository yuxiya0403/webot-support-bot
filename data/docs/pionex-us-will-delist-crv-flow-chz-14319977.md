# Pionex.US Will Delist CRV, FLOW, CHZ
> Dear Users,To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to ...
Dear Users,

To improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will delist the tokens and their relevant trading pairs related to the following tokens **at 08:00 on July 20th, 2023 (UTC)**:

**Tokens: CRV, FLOW, CHZ**

**Please Note**

- Delisting Trading Pairs:

- **CRV/USD**

- **FLOW/USDT**

- **CHZ/USD**

- **CHZ/USDT**

- At the same time as the above trading pairs are delisted, the bot orders and manual orders of the corresponding trading pairs will be canceled.

- ****CRV, FLOW, CHZ ** **** **positions in the bot orders will be released to the trading account after the relevant bot orders are canceled.

- Due to the Mainnet factors of the above-mentioned tokens, Pionex.US will not be able to support the withdrawal features with the relevant tokens. The **CRV, FLOW, and CHZ** positions will be sold after the delisting, and the USDT will be issued to the users' holding accounts within 30 business days. If you are worried about price fluctuations, closing the bot in advance or selling them manually is recommended.
