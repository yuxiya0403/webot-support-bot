What is a phone-based attack?

A phone-based attack (SIM-swap or phone-port attack) is when an attacker has their target's phone number transferred to a mobile device under the attacker's control. Fraudsters do this through various means, including identity theft and socially engineering mobile carrier customer-support representatives. This attack threatens all accounts using SMS-based 2-step verification and any account that can be recovered using phone-based authentication.

How can I prevent a phone-based attack?

To help protect your Pionex.US account from this attack, we highly recommend using a stronger form of [2-factor authentication](https://pionexus.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/19694783485081), such as a Time-based One Time Password (TOTP) with a mobile authenticator app like Google Authenticator.
