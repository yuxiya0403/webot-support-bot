# Why does my bank show that the payment has been successfully deducted, but the order status is still 'pending'?

**Answer**: Although your bank has confirmed the successful deduction, we require time to receive and process this confirmation. This typically involves the **ACH** settlement process, which may take **4 business days**. During this period, the order status will remain "pending." Please be patient, we will update your order status as soon as we receive confirmation. If the status remains unchanged for an extended period, please contact customer support (service@pionex.us) for further assistance.
