# Pionex.US Will Delist BAND/USD Trading Pair
> Dear Users,Due to the poor liquidity of  BAND/USD, Pionex.US will delist the BAND/USD  trading pair at 06:00 on November 13th, 2023 (UTC)....
Dear Users,

Due to the poor liquidity of** BAND/USD**, Pionex.US will delist the **BAND/USD** ** **trading pair** at 06:00 on November 13th, 2023 (UTC)**. Users can trade **BAND** with the **BAND/USDT **trading pair.

**Please Note**

- **This announcement only affects registered users before December 28th,2022 (UTC).**

- Pionex.US will delist the **BAND****/USD** trading pair and cancel the related manual orders and bot orders, and the **BAND**** **positions will be released to the trading account.

- Users can trade **BAND**** ** with the **BAND****/USDT**** ** trading pair after delisting the **BAND****/USD ** trading pair.
