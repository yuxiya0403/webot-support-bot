Mobile App operation step

Please click **[Home] **-** [Deposit] **-** [History] **-** [Wire] **to check the **[****Withdrawable Time]**.

​​​​

![](https://static.intercomassets.com/assets/article_broken_image_placeholder.png)

Website operation step

1. After logging in to the account, click **[Wallet]** - **[Overview]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218017371/21289eabc4dc666f2270eaf09935/19722443421209?expires=1776232800&signature=a01351f92dc9290361b731f855c0a5e7ab2982da0da4a764544749a67515b044&req=diImHsl%2FmoJYWPMW3nq%2Bgf2zD6qxUVq5oFKKE%2Bczf9pQLeOg6%2FgmUpjn7xpb%0AI6jW1l%2F4mfJGO1IJoxygOddOjPU%3D%0A)
