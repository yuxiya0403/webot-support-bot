# TRX Network Wallet Maintenance Announcement
Dear Users,

Webot will be conducting scheduled upgrade maintenance on the **TRX**(TRON)** **wallet on **June**** 06th, 2024, at 06:50 (UTC).**

Please note the following:

- This announcement only affects registered users **before December 28th, 2022 (UTC).**

- During the wallet maintenance period, trading functions related to **TRX **network tokens will not be affected.

- Pionex will temporarily suspend the deposit service for **TRX** network tokens starting from **June 06th, 2024, at 06:50 (UTC).**

- During the suspension period, deposits of **TRX** will not be credited.

- Webot will resume the deposit services for **TRX** network tokens once the maintenance is completed and the network stabilizes. Further announcements will not be made accordingly.

Trading cryptocurrencies carries significant risks. Please exercise caution when purchasing and be mindful of trading risks. While Webot carefully selects quality cryptocurrencies, we do not guarantee, compensate, or assume responsibility for any of your trading activities.
