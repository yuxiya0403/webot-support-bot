# Webot now supports  deposit/withdrawal of ETH (Optimism) and ETH (ARBITRUM)
Dear users,

Webot now supports deposit/withdrawal of ETH (Optimism) and ETH (ARBITRUM)

Related parameters:

- ETH (Optimism) minimum deposit amount: 0.001 ETH, withdrawal fee: 0.0005 ETH;

- ETH (Arbitrum) minimum deposit amount: 0.001 ETH, withdrawal fee: 0.0002 ETH;

**Please note:**

- If your deposit amount is lower than the minimum deposit amount, you may not receive this deposit and lost the fund.

Trading in the crypto market carries a substantial risk and the possibility of making both significant profits and losses. Please trade cautiously.
