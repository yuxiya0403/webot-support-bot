# Webot Account Registration Guide
> This guide covers the registration process for individual accounts. If you are registering on behalf of a company or institution, please refer to the Institutional Account Registration Guide.
Welcome to Webot! Beginning your cryptocurrency journey is an important milestone, and registering a Webot account is the first step toward accessing a compliant and advanced suite of automated trading bots.

This guide provides a comprehensive, step-by-step overview of the account registration process, including required documentation, identity verification, security setup, and funding your account.

**1. Pre-Registration Checklist**

Before registering, please ensure that you meet the following requirements and have the necessary information available.

**i. U.S. Residency Requirement**

Webot services are available only to residents of the United States.

**ii. Valid Government-Issued Identification**

You must provide one of the following valid identification documents:

- U.S. Driver’s License

- State Identification Card

- U.S. Passport or Passport Card

_If a Passport or Passport Card is used, additional Proof of Address verification is required._

For detailed documentation requirements, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119734-what-documents-are-required-for-your-verification) to learn more.

**iii. Social Security Number (SSN) or Tax Identification Number (TIN)**

To comply with U.S. tax reporting and regulatory requirements, all users must complete SSN or TIN verification.

For instructions on completing SSN/TIN verification, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119779-social-security-number-or-taxpayer-identification-number-ssn-tin-verification-faq) to learn more.

**iv. Supported State of Residence**

Users must reside in a state where Webot services are supported in order to complete identity verification.

To review the list of unsupported states, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119735-list-of-unsupported-states) to check.

**v. Not a U.S. Resident?**

Unfortunately, Webot is currently unable to provide services to users outside the United States. Any future adjustments or updates will be communicated to users in a timely manner.

**2. Create Your Account**

You can easily register for a Webot account with your email address or Apple/Google account on Webot in just a few clicks.

**WEB:**

1. Go to the [Webot website](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and click **[Register]** in the upper right corner or select a registration method directly. You can sign up with your email address, Apple, or Google account.

_

**Sign up with your email address:**

2. Enter your email address and secure password for your account. The password should be at least 8 characters and include both letters and numbers. If you’ve been referred to register on Webot by a friend, make sure to fill in their Referral ID (optional). 
​
Then, read and agree to the User Agreement, Privacy Policy, Risk Disclaimer, and User Conduct Code, and click the **[Create Account]** button to continue.

_

3. After completing the slider verification, you will receive a 6-digit verification code in your email. Enter the code within 8 minutes and click the **[Confirm]** button.

_

4. Congratulations! You have successfully created a personal Webot account.

**Sign up with your Apple/Google account:**

2. Select **[Continue with Google]** or **[Continue with Apple]**. A pop-up window will appear, prompting you to sign in to Webot using your Apple or Google account.

_

3. After signing in, you will be asked to confirm before proceeding.

4. You’ll be redirected back to the Webot website. If you’ve been referred to register on Webot by a friend, make sure to fill in their Referral ID (optional).

Then, read and agree to the User Agreement, Privacy Policy, Risk Disclaimer, and User Conduct Code, and click the **[Next step]** button.

_

5. Congratulations! You have successfully created a Webot account.

**APP:**

1. Download the [Webot application](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download) and go to the “Account” click **[Create account]**. You can sign up with your email address, Apple, or Google account.

_

**Sign up with your email address:**

2. Enter your email address and click **[Next step]**. You will receive a 6-digit verification code in your email. Enter the code within 8 minutes and click the **[Confirm]** button.

3. Secure a password for your account. The password should be at least 8 characters and include both letters and numbers. If you’ve been referred to register on Webot by a friend, make sure to fill in their Referral ID (optional). 

_By clicking “Confirm,” you are deemed to have agreed to the Terms & Service. Please read them before clicking._

_

4. Congratulations! You have successfully created a personal Webot account.

**Sign up with your Apple/Google account:**

2. Select **[Continue with Google]** or **[Continue with Apple]** to register using your existing account credentials.

_

3. After signing in, you will be asked to confirm before proceeding.

4. If you’ve been referred to register on Webot by a friend, make sure to fill in their Referral ID (optional) and click the **[Next step]** button.

_By clicking “Confirm,” you are deemed to have agreed to the Terms & Service. Please read them before clicking._

_

5. Congratulations! You have successfully created a Webot account.

**3. Complete Identity Verification (KYC)**

All Webot users are required to complete identity verification before accessing platform services, including deposits, trading, and withdrawals.

⚠️ _Accounts without completed identity verification will be unable to deposit funds, execute trades, or withdraw assets._

Please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119787-how-to-complete-identity-verification) to learn how to complete identity verification.

**4. Secure Your Account**

Account security is critical when trading digital assets. We strongly recommend completing the following security measures.

**Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

- Navigate to Account → Security

- Link all Email, Phone Number, and Google Authenticator

- Adds layers of protection beyond your password

**Enable Withdrawal Whitelisting**

- Restricts withdrawals to pre-approved wallet addresses only

- Helps prevent unauthorized asset transfers

**Enable Security Notifications**

- Turn on email or SMS notifications for login attempts, withdrawals, and security setting changes

- Prompt alerts allow you to respond quickly to potential unauthorized activity

**Protect Your Personal Devices**

- Keep your operating system and applications up to date

- Use secure internet connections and avoid public Wi-Fi when accessing your account

We strongly recommend binding all available security verification methods, including your email address, mobile phone number, and Google Authenticator. Enabling multiple verification methods adds additional layers of protection and helps ensure smoother account recovery when needed.

**5. Fund Your Account**

After successfully completing the identity verification process, you will be able to add funds to your account. Once your account is funded, you can start engaging in trading activities immediately. This verification step is essential to ensure the security and compliance of your account before any transactions can take place.

**Deposit USD**

- Webot supports USD deposits via **Debit Card, ACH Bank Transfer, and Wire Transfer**. Connect your bank account or card to fund your account securely.

For detailed instructions, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119738-usd-deposit-methods-on-webot) to review the USD deposit guide.

**Deposit Cryptocurrency**

- Transfer supported cryptocurrencies from an external wallet or exchange to your Webot account by using your assigned deposit address (e.g., BTC, USDT).

For step-by-step instructions, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119837-how-to-receive-crypto-on-webot) to review the Cryptocurrency deposit guide.

**6. Start Trading**

Once your account is funded, you may commence trading and explore the automated trading bots and strategies offered on Webot.

For detailed, step-by-step instructions on executing your first trade and utilizing trading bots, please click [here](https://intercom.help/webot/en/collections/19059921-bots) to access the trading tutorial.

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
