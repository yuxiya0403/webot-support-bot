# How To Convert Small Account Balance to USDT
Trading often leaves behind "dust"—minuscule balances remaining in your wallet after a transaction. At Webot, you can efficiently consolidate these small assets (valued below 10 USDT) into USDT once every 6 hours.

**On Web:**

- Navigate to **[Wallet]** > **[Overview]**.

- Click on **[Dust Collector]**.

- Select the specific assets you wish to exchange and click **[Convert]**.

- Click **[CONFIRM]** to finalize the conversion.

_

**On App:**

- Navigate to the **[Account]** section.

- Select **[Dust Collector]**.

- Choose your assets and tap **[Convert]** to complete the redemption.

- Click **[CONFIRM]** to finalize the conversion.

_

**Note:** To review your past transactions, simply click on **[Collector History]**.

_

Please review the following guidelines to ensure a smooth conversion process:

- **Eligibility:** An asset is automatically categorized as a "small balance" when its valuation falls below 10 USDT.

- **Visibility:** If small balances are not appearing in your wallet, go to [More Settings] in the app and disable the "Hide small balance" toggle.

- **Conversion Frequency:** You may perform one small balance conversion every 6 hours.

- **Asset Limitations:** Currently, the Dust Collector only supports conversion into USDT. For conversions to USD, please utilize the "**[SWAP](https://intercom.help/webot/en/articles/14119731-how-to-convert-cryptocurrency)"** feature.

- Unlisted or delisted tokens are ineligible for conversion.

- **Market Pricing:** The conversion value provided by the system is a real-time estimate. Webot executes the final exchange based on prevailing market prices at the time of the transaction.

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
