# How to Sign in via QR Code

Logging into your Pionex.US account via QR codes is a secure and convenient method through your mobile app. Here's a quick guide:

**1. Access Pionex.US Sign-In Page**

- Open your web browser and visit the [Pionex.US sign-in page](https://accounts.pionex.us/en/sign).

**2. Generate Your QR Code**

- Once on the sign-in page, your unique QR code will be automatically generated and displayed. This QR code is your key to quick and secure access.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011910/efbb0299b85304aba9bcc8172bd6/23778272640665?expires=1776232800&signature=ed2467701fbe0bdd022e6347cd6462766b6af0c88710946b2dcd814a180b1112&req=diImHsl%2FnIheWfMW3nq%2BgbyBfZGcRrgP9XbeETgwuO4csSfIeK8HGkUDOv9x%0AxnZ7QGLleFABFqbhcGcZi2XpnqE%3D%0A)

Note – The QR code in your web browser is valid for 2 minutes. If it expires, click "**Refresh**" for a new QR code

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011938/f84ab37d64a24c79b21546dd0f52/24089672923673?expires=1776232800&signature=b12dd2fa8292b88b9a37626d94df5daebabd8d2c524251d221dd105ea7112efa&req=diImHsl%2FnIhcUfMW3nq%2BgS0Smw4cFAgXnlzRMU%2BYM1KEVNVSkTaH%2BTWsUeca%0AEZLsHRu6RZF1yh9yPD%2Ftlt5hKtE%3D%0A)

**3. Scan the QR Code**

To proceed, follow these steps in your Pionex.US mobile app:

- Open the Pionex.US mobile app and go to the "**Home**" screen.

- Look for the QR code scan icon, located in the top right corner of the app.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218011986/b7f84ccc387ae73fdc7f6656e84e/23780325156249?expires=1776232800&signature=0248716c9699afe4ad7dfa2770bafe657cb9ac2e303ae9dd3a111831da4b8400&req=diImHsl%2FnIhXX%2FMW3nq%2BgSJnwJEOy5jyLqG1p8Wh6pgdgyUhK7HcczhfbUW%2F%0A34kR0RYtgcKKzbqVHWGFgZE7cpw%3D%0A)

- Use your phone's camera to scan the QR code displayed on your web browser.

- If prompted, grant camera access to the app.

- After successfully scanning, tap 'Authorize' within the mobile app.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218012007/1c848d186010d0ccea3dd1d55020/24035621673113?expires=1776232800&signature=0aec08c3cef48268bf89caaf0584f5b601b0c179862b1cf000a6958daaf66d8d&req=diImHsl%2Fn4FfXvMW3nq%2Bgd6BHpsJqm%2FqZsSF8UkbDN4VyIDaDWZcQ7qWkW6k%0AViyeCiP8gG9w63RhieHEKnoqlYk%3D%0A)

That's it! With these straightforward steps, you'll gain instant access to your Pionex.US account through your web browser. Enjoy the ease and security of QR code logins for seamless account management.
