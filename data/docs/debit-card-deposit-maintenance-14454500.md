# Debit Card Deposit Maintenance
Dear Users,

Our Debit Card Deposit function is currently under maintenance **from 18:00 EST on November 15th, 2023**. We will keep you informed and publish further announcement once the maintenance is completed.

During the maintenance, the Debit Card Deposit function will be temporarily unavailable, and users will not be able to add new Debit Cards. After the maintenance is completed, the Debit Card deposit function will be restored, and pending Debit Card deposits will be will be automatically credited to users' accounts.
