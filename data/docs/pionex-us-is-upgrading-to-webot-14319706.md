Dear Valued Users,

We're excited to announce that Pionex.US is evolving into **Webot** – a brand upgrade that represents our commitment to delivering superior service quality and enhanced regulatory compliance.

**What This Means for You**

This rebrand is about improving your experience. With Webot, you can expect:

- Enhanced platform services and user experience

- Stronger compliance and security standards

- Continued innovation in cryptocurrency trading

**Your Account Remains Secure**

Rest assured, this transition will not affect your daily operations. Your funds, trading history, account settings, and login credentials all remain unchanged. All services continue without interruption, and no action is required from you.

**Looking Ahead**

Webot represents our vision for the future – providing you with a trusted, compliant, and user-focused platform for all your cryptocurrency needs. Thank you for being part of this journey with us.

If you have any questions, our support team is available 24/7 to assist you.

Best regards,
Webot Team (Formerly Pionex.US)
