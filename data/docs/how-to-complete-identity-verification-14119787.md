# How to Complete Identity Verification
> You can submit your Identity verification application by following these steps on the Webot website or app.👉🏻 App operation step👉🏻 Web ope...
You can submit your Identity verification application by following these steps on the Webot website or app.

👉🏻 **[App operation step](#h_01KFB22EYKNWAV2WKFSB0Q2KP3)**

👉🏻 **[Web operation step](#h_01KFB2KVS99V2JCVKWKNDVZJTB)**

App operation step

**Step 1:** Open the [Webot app](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/download) and click [Home] - [Avatar Icon] - [Security] - [Identity verification].

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933447/b34691a2eaa90610e7dcb48b5726/14927901025167?expires=1776232800&signature=303846f8926049d6a6c9a91ba8de365a641816e986a9298953f9909ee00cfdd5&req=diEgEcB9noVbXvMW3nq%2BgVVECAo6V6YQ6P6NKuq4sXxVreN36DIEBs04aWHD%0AuXs3uBYutvcGMQ684jIh3DUc%2Bpw%3D%0A)

**Step 2:** Click [Verify], then [Verify] again, select your document type, and click [Next step] to proceed.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933496/034bbc8323970febf119f459e9bd/14927915261711?expires=1776232800&signature=aca7d01a1c289b4ad16afcd5d447017d5c2fbea9bd6fd2272b81b1fb6cde2a85&req=diEgEcB9noVWX%2FMW3nq%2BgYS8UcasnfAVmrzf9llqAEVp9KBQ7tI5YGiXezYZ%0AEsxM08LM%2F9XVpjmHdddPALLferQ%3D%0A)

**Step 3:** Click [Continue], then select the ID document–issuing country and choose the document type.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933542/8761e6a0021b14146064742365ac/14927901025295?expires=1776232800&signature=87ff103113ed1e06e941bc224450064cedd2c8ceede5a5d6a9d508a6675783be&req=diEgEcB9noRbW%2FMW3nq%2BgRWMg%2Bzda%2B2RmUiejvnY0i2qgGt4MMpQ3vPoknvt%0AcY0h32fejEFAPD1pKDAf6IE2ag8%3D%0A)

**Step 4:** Enable/allow camera permissions when prompted, take clear, unmodified photos of your documents, and click the [Document is readable] button to submit them.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933572/db1248aae679bcb20a1eb594131e/14927901025423?expires=1776232800&signature=b59da660416001f94e0d32e9f63f64c54249054f20d69139e0c3c0a90391fd1c&req=diEgEcB9noRYW%2FMW3nq%2BgRNJX8FYlGmb5H1dfZeoTBEEBblaYkj2JgHWiY5C%0AwdKs1Za%2B4JKnoSrUveMTC6bD4OU%3D%0A)

**Step 5:** Click the [Continue] button, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the liveness check, and submit your application.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933612/90ea31e8aff66cbca4901046cbae/14927915264655?expires=1776232800&signature=4a76fa48da14732d229a28373e32859211315304b7a254112209253b6d569ae9&req=diEgEcB9nodeW%2FMW3nq%2BgTzaOh9LTzDd7btPOkd%2FI1LhgMRob6ZKinDDvCTt%0AF53YltDcCdR6yDW%2BeyvyOS31sWM%3D%0A)

Web operation step

**Step 1:** Log in to your [Webot account](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US/sign), click [Avatar Icon] - [Account Settings] - [Apply] - [Verify].

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933630/2a22d43bc60d9ed5e60bec0151cd/14927915266703?expires=1776232800&signature=f831da67168b8586e0f4647878b01badb1439a4f46a9d9ec6beaca09c1ceba88&req=diEgEcB9nodcWfMW3nq%2BgYajXMBbnazggLp%2Fvn3Q7fi04U5F9b69hVfNp0i3%0AcRf0ZZ4B8IDjeI5gyl8vtWuhYEI%3D%0A)

**Step 2:** Select your country of residence, enter your SSN/TIN, choose the document type you wish to upload, then click [Next] - [Continue].

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933660/a7d073028c12a9b6719d84d77f8d/14927901025807?expires=1776232800&signature=8376fa6a891a4959965ba393789f2732f45665725a19d9572bcec2f2a3c90327&req=diEgEcB9nodZWfMW3nq%2BgblbDNch%2FSJLctFO2N0Ptc%2BtAnTKFY%2FKxaNlyN1a%0AQQj7Zv4xlzxbXx8hNkAyePOaSFo%3D%0A)

**Step 3**: After reviewing the information, click [Agree and continue] to give your consent, then click [Start verification].

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933680/f450bf7c948e161dc6f371edbf81/14927901025935?expires=1776232800&signature=45e7b8919215a524cfb1d8b845564d3d4271a3d229bc3f9211010e3d27cb8ab5&req=diEgEcB9nodXWfMW3nq%2BgcuFltoGnvywkYXGLpYuwTQIpYqJEvHlK5VzlRlu%0AmjSX5hAGBDMCpz6ar8lHQ20fQXw%3D%0A)

**Step 4**: Select the ID document–issuing country and choose the document type. Click [Continue], submit clear, unmodified photos of your documents, and then click [Continue].

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933705/eaf5688c3a912bbb3dcb588220a4/14927901026191?expires=1776232800&signature=15ac6a00dd997230151897986027fdd5456f22f8ef41c8e1665969aad26addfe&req=diEgEcB9noZfXPMW3nq%2BgSFFqrPkbg5obMK2P2baFOANGb9A56cGC0%2B6tHCh%0AdZy%2BRF6NIFRVitrb0Or%2Bh3QqR9c%3D%0A)

**Step 5**: After reading the tips, click [Continue] - [Continue]. Enable/allow camera permissions when prompted, and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the liveness check and submit your application.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177933739/d9d081a410fcca9e4f0059a399c5/14927915270543?expires=1776232800&signature=f191fc213329e5238a2ad62e9e3283509e669137b41ea7aba9fed1bd09b6f3c0&req=diEgEcB9noZcUPMW3nq%2BgfDbWmZ571y7ptLFcejS7hyJtNlHMdenN%2F6NaT0m%0AZeucXQ9H9hewFo61Pcxi7MwKIsg%3D%0A)

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
