How to Buy Crypto on the Mobile App

1. Tap the **[Buy/Sell]** button on the bottom of your screen. On the Buy/Sell screen, select **[Buy]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016259/414002fffddf784eec1094d3ff3e/19018988155417?expires=1776232800&signature=7935555f62efa9d04db19fa8dcfd77354ec30767e2f6517722d48220e1a76a07&req=diImHsl%2Fm4NaUPMW3nq%2BgS88Jx%2Fsb%2BTht0S1xwc5lVrB6yniPTRWWTrQS%2BCg%0AEb5CN53eRcckL%2BgGt5nJX7gy%2FRY%3D%0A)

2. Tap the coin symbol next to the "Choose Asset" line and select the coin you want to buy.

After selecting your asset, use the number pad to enter the amount of the asset you would like to purchase (in the USD equivalent).

Your current payment method will show under the USD purchase amount. If you'd like to change methods, tap the arrow to the right of the current method. You can then add a new payment method or select from previously added methods.

Once satisfied with your order, tap **[Buy] **to proceed.

After confirming that the information regarding your transaction is correct, tap **[****Confirm] **to execute your order

_Note: If you can't find your desired cryptocurrency in this menu but Pionex.US support it, please use the regular Pionex.US [**trade screen**.](https://v1.pionex.us/en-US/trade/BTC_USDT/Manual)_

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016290/e43e9d12160b9f35db0496dc6d06/19019021827609?expires=1776232800&signature=b857576acba25bf2ef7d498bf5370ec602b917ac13ffda609e8706042865efc7&req=diImHsl%2Fm4NWWfMW3nq%2Bgas9LcoI5yT4YA%2F38eK3Ey6XsNaPrOM%2Fy9FBv5FW%0AVaJmwIwAa3en7gkdG%2BR5%2FEc2M3w%3D%0A)

3. Your buy order will now be processed, and you should see a screen showing that your buy was successful. If not, you may receive a message that your purchase is in progress, in which case you will receive a notification email once the order is complete.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016306/a673b51b2243a67a1603632c74d6/18959310123545?expires=1776232800&signature=419ee07335719fe5347b886fcd987c384f5e66a80476c3590e58e0948a70058b&req=diImHsl%2Fm4JfX%2FMW3nq%2BgWr%2BYhEHo8ErO3LuCgLbgzFJLhuBbEoSAmb4%2BDQP%0A%2F8j9BpyxfGfElOmf0FmQvqKJExc%3D%0A)

How to Buy Crypto on the Website

1. After logging into your account, click the **[****Buy Crypto] **button on the toolbar at the top of the screen.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016327/c45ab4174227a61055a5533615dc/19019012990361?expires=1776232800&signature=5adf0c7509057e69866b2b8c428f12a77e5900d4be5d6ffdd30fe39a297eaa18&req=diImHsl%2Fm4JdXvMW3nq%2BgTxkbzeqZRuvJpPahtd5DYZuoWlXzrmngwP30Hax%0AGQmds13avCYqDDPyFTnuzRKok%2FA%3D%0A)

2. Click the cryptocurrency shown in the buy panel next to the "Choose asset" text. Select the cryptocurrency you would like to purchase from the drop-down menu.

In the "Enter amount" box, enter the USD amount you want to purchase the cryptocurrency. Please note that all orders must be at least 2.00 USD. When you are done, click **[Bu****y]_._**

If you are satisfied, click **[Confirm] **to execute your order. Please note you will pay from your USD balance in your Pionex.US wallet by default.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016362/7d2ecff58fcbc644e3aac17d5e46/19020146362777?expires=1776232800&signature=46ae0f5ea8bdfa47d8c1fe2477591fc0b4f623243c325074566f4ebac7213d81&req=diImHsl%2Fm4JZW%2FMW3nq%2BgW%2FkQOHPRzXS3WwgunBM66DvfA0%2FttHL5OU2sm3E%0AH%2FxIYPA5lrsQFGS8533BzBo6bwY%3D%0A)

3. A message will pop up showing that your buy was successful. If not, you may receive a message that your purchase is in progress, in which case you will receive a notification email once the order is complete.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016410/80922d3c9d2bad94562c0e2e54bf/19019045754777?expires=1776232800&signature=2c31daacc62e6a6c8c3a079e2229a53cabb708fdff40f78916602f20792fd859&req=diImHsl%2Fm4VeWfMW3nq%2BgR0YAxLPrBQ14PjddhkNFlGktX%2BjYuM8a4PwjS9z%0Amkgqqo%2Bu0atspIu08AJ9SLdPxXY%3D%0A)

How to Sell Crypto on the Website

1. After logging into your account, click the **[Trade] **button on the toolbar at the top of the screen.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016622/721299f9e3941a7137481e4794c2/19019147096345?expires=1776232800&signature=e904dd67ef8649649501709993dde8331dbbb261c0076020db9dd0fc95564c83&req=diImHsl%2Fm4ddW%2FMW3nq%2BgUvEMYjLZk4agQFmkeMctx3VLfa5hzf4yrufj8I%2F%0A%2BUsq4rF5Xl73ii8e%2FKOkkiCvXPQ%3D%0A)

2. Click on the **[****Convert]** tab in the panel on the left-hand side of your screen.

Click the cryptocurrencies shown in the Convert panel on your screen. Select the top cryptocurrency. You would like to sell this currency from the drop-down menu.

Then click the bottom cryptocurrency. This is the currency that you would like to sell to.

Click the** [Quantity]** sign below the top cryptocurrency to enter the amount of the initial cryptocurrency you wish to sell to the other. When you are finished, click **[****Convert].**

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016663/747ecb200974b3ab07106a9aec21/19020332800537?expires=1776232800&signature=997bfb6e61595dcfbd6e01731b0cb41300469cd28083f1d6af2ffb2f0d7aa68d&req=diImHsl%2Fm4dZWvMW3nq%2BgR71LEke11JXseTyqKHQQY1jQVbQ3KAC8Av2tLzh%0AHIKmeTxIVv1gcy30pJ9tLBpjU0s%3D%0A)

3. If everything was completed correctly, you should see your transaction listed as **"****Successful Swap!"** at the top of the screen.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016699/03e5bb33a1ddeed38684d3114503/19019103746585?expires=1776232800&signature=b03e2d572a874ffc7c6d83292c36028a67b7dd124a4330881da785081c6b9853&req=diImHsl%2Fm4dWUPMW3nq%2BgdawSAWP%2FdDhlCe%2FP8fyUu9%2FCKnrvu1gjWGdLeL4%0AWkAdqNApEygeeDKkNIc1PGLFw%2BA%3D%0A)

How to Convert Crypto on the Mobile App

1. Tap the **[****Buy/Sell]** button on the bottom of your screen. On the Buy/Sell screen, select **[****Convert].**

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016742/0c3914ee6895a4e088bb6b4ea369/19019205773081?expires=1776232800&signature=2d4f651d37f2dff8f194acd7372919d529d4269ae8177e9917bdb6e5496eeca8&req=diImHsl%2Fm4ZbW%2FMW3nq%2BgfBQlHnktOaRd4EcnAPR1BOPdPYtiLiqmGbbqv2C%0AjX3q1%2FLntOT33yd0pYLC1Ue0Aco%3D%0A)

2. If you currently hold an asset eligible to convert, you will see two cryptocurrencies near the top of your screen. Tap the top cryptocurrency symbol next to **"****From"** to select the asset you want to convert. Then, tap the bottom cryptocurrency symbol next to **"****To"** to select the asset you want to convert.

Alternatively, you can tap the arrows to swap places between the current "To" and "From" assets.

You can use the number pad to enter the amount of the first asset you want to convert into the second asset. After you confirm that the information about your conversion is correct, please tap **[****Convert] **to execute your order.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016805/35b3bd7fa690eb09fe1decfba021/19019174365849?expires=1776232800&signature=4fbcb7e4644b3f8166c6026ad73b0f7b5474fdc1876b01b16271b3b50b00676d&req=diImHsl%2Fm4lfXPMW3nq%2BgcVcDfmRzdHzn7nkz%2FN1fHNPTIhESd%2B9PvsnST0E%0ALXz%2FkaJ1ZkPAAwiJOaV69uF6yo0%3D%0A)

3. Your conversion order will now be processed, and you should see a screen showing your successful conversion. If not, you may receive a message that your order is in progress, in which case you will receive a notification email once the order is complete.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016829/43a63c1cd38b18f1467704b66675/18959153380633?expires=1776232800&signature=09a1299e8386345136738d32d09bdf86b283e7070fb4b0c0523cac74502ffa18&req=diImHsl%2Fm4ldUPMW3nq%2BgfhmAmqJyk8XDriLeD80Rse5cr6eKfrlT0YxAb9D%0ALM8gfF7CjUT89zQX2ig0pgKs3Xg%3D%0A)

1. After logging into your account, click the **[Trade] **button on the toolbar at the top of the screen.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218016887/05fe47b02c42e2c37ff88b02d991/20340965644697?expires=1776232800&signature=1f66075b5bcead540a35fbf90e2afaed4216dcf368ea71044b77e328357c116a&req=diImHsl%2Fm4lXXvMW3nq%2BgW9hnn7fzluSBaB0dBBb%2BbuQiJzh4JasPUctkpDE%0AvtZbtVr3xcJC4I9br4rZiAuwaJc%3D%0A)

Alternatively, you can also tap the arrows in between to swap places between the current "From" and "To" assets.
