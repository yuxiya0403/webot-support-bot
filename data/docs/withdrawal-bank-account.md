# How to Withdraw USD to Bank Account

Withdrawing funds from your Webot account is simple and secure. This guide walks you through the process of transferring USD from your trading account directly into your bank account.

Step 1: Verify Your Account details

Before you can withdraw USD, ensure that your Webot account is fully verified and the documents are up-to-date and not within the final 1-month of expiry:

If you had been using Webot and verified using a ducment with an expiry date such as:

- State Driver's license (front and back)

- Federal/State-issued ID card (front and back)

You may reach out to Webot customer support to allow you to resubmit your latest document to ensure your withdrawal experience process are smooth.

Step 2: Check if your funds has withdrawal holding time from USD deposits

If you have recently deposited your account through USD deposit methods such as Bank Account (ACH), Debit Card, and Wire Transfer, each of the deposit methods has varying deposit holding time and this will prevents you from being able to withdraw the funds. Best to check your withdrawable time before making any asset conversion or withdrawals. Refer to the article to check the steps to check: https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868792660239

Once everything checks out, you are ready to convert assets into USD.

Step 3: Convert the crypto assets into USD

Before you can withdraw the USD, all traded assets are in their respective currencies, if you bought BTC, your assets is in BTC eventhough your account dashboard indicates your account is USD($) denomination, it is calculated alltogether, hence the assets need to be converted into USD.

- Navigate to the following path

- [APP: Buy/Sell → Lite → Convert](http://link.pionex.us/?page=webview&autoJump=true&url=https://www.webot.com/en-US/buy-crypto/convert)

- [WEB: Buy Crypto → Convert](http://link.pionex.us/?page=webview&autoJump=true&url=https://www.webot.com/en-US/buy-crypto/convert).

- Convert the assets that you have into USD

- Refer to the Convert article to learn how to convert your cryptocurrency: [https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868695066511](https://webot.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14868695066511)

- After the conversion into USD is complete, you funds are ready to be withdrawn.
