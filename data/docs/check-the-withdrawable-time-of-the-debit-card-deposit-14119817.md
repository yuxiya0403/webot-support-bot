# Check the "Withdrawable Time" of the Debit Card deposit
> You can check your Debit Card deposit's "Withdrawable Time" by following the steps.
> 👉🏻 App operation step
> 👉🏻 Web operation step
> Mobile App...
You can check your Debit Card deposit's "Withdrawable Time" by following the steps.

👉🏻 [**App operation step**](#h_01H36XV1PHDB2AQAZF3C5HA824)

👉🏻[** Web operation step**](#h_01H36XV64XMWRMPT40KRPZNEEE)

Mobile App operation step

Please click **[Home] - [Deposit] - [History] - [Card] **to check the **[****Withdrawable Time]**.
​

Website operation step

1. After logging in to the account, click **[Wallet]** - **[Overview]**.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177995436/e3b7368af32947300ef0d977b8ab/19722268149529?expires=1776232800&signature=4b467946e947f38180221256babfd252ff51dcbb17ef81b0705fdaf04483cb1d&req=diEgEcB3mIVcX%2FMW3nq%2BgYaUX9iu42XhBdlDq9IiZ1cwgFdKjTpV2EPT3mql%0AaZd%2BUzeVebwcGReaXcomIQkMMow%3D%0A)

2. Click **[USD]** under the **"Deposit"** tab.
​

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177995484/5d4488ecafcb7515c05efdd5d336/19722268152345?expires=1776232800&signature=0d5d555180989ce8e9459a4e9cda8c5877bf06f398bd2ca0299edd1875f26f0f&req=diEgEcB3mIVXXfMW3nq%2BgU2NkYmiVS%2FKgaglJtBb6q5YqzhqMfLYQOyg9%2FFv%0AlC4etT%2Bq9UQk0j51GAPm%2B6wF7%2F8%3D%0A)

3. In the **"Debit Card"** tab, click **[Order History]** - **[Withdrawable Time]**.
