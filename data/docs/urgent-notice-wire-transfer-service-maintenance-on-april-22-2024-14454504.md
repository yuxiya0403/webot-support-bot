# Urgent Notice: Wire transfer service Maintenance on April 22, 2024
Dear User,

We'd like to inform you that we will conduct essential system maintenance on the Wire transfer service to boost our service and efficiency. Please note the important details below:

1. The maintenance is set to start at 23:59 (PST) on April 22, 2024. Wire transfer service will be unavailable.
2. Please note that deposits and withdrawals made during the maintenance may result in asset accessibility that Webot is unable to recover for you. To sidestep potential issues, you can choose to deposit or withdraw through other available channels.

3. After the completion of maintenance, we will release a new announcement to inform.

We apologize for any inconvenience it may cause and thank you for your support. For any questions, please contact us: at service@webot.com.
