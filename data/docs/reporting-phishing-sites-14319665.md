# Reporting phishing sites

Phishing sites are malicious websites that mimic an authentic site to trick visitors into entering their login credentials and other sensitive information. One of the best ways to avoid phishing sites is always to make sure you're accessing the official website of Pionex.US directly and not a different web address.

Please note that these are all the official websites of Pionex.US:

- [https://v1.pionex.us/en-US/](https://v1.pionex.us/en-US/)

- [https://www.pionex.us/en-US/](https://www.pionex.us/en-US/)

- [https://accounts.pionex.us/en/](https://accounts.pionex.us/en/)

Report phishing emails

If you believe you've encountered a phishing site, please email service@pionex.us with the full URL.

If the phish was emailed, please include full email headers with your report. Email headers show the network path that an email took to your inbox. Without them, Pionex.US cannot complete a full investigation as we cannot identify which mail server is involved.

To collect email headers, please reference your email provider's support documents or review this webpage: [https://mxtoolbox.com/public/content/emailheaders/](https://mxtoolbox.com/public/content/emailheaders/) to find instructions for your specific email client.

You should read and understand all applicable terms for MxToolbox before using them._

Report phishing texts to Pionex.US

If the phishing message was sent via text message or SMS, please submit a screenshot of the phishing text to service@pionex.us. You can also reduce the likelihood of receiving messages like this in the future by copying and pasting the contents into a new SMS message and sending it to 7726 (SPAM).

When we receive your report, our security team will investigate your submission and take prompt action to shut down any malicious sites targeting Pionex.US customers.

Report phishing to your mobile provider

If you use a US-based cell phones carrier like AT&T, Verizon, Sprint, or T-Mobile, you can help reduce phishing attacks by sending the contents of suspicious SMS messages to **7726 (SPAM)**. This free service allows mobile carriers to detect and block malicious messages on their network.

Thank you for helping keep Pionex.US and our customers safe from phishing sites!
