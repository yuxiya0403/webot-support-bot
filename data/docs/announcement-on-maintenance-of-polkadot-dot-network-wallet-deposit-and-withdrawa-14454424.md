# Announcement on Maintenance of Polkadot (DOT) Network Wallet Deposit and Withdrawal Functions
**Dear Users:**

Webot will suspend the token deposit and withdrawal functions of the Polkadot (DOT) network at **02:00 on November 4, 2025 (UTC)**, and support the network migration to Asset Hub Polkadot.

**Notes:**

- During the maintenance period, this cryptocurrency and related network tokens can be traded normally.

- Deposit and withdrawal services will be reopened after maintenance is completed without further notice.

Thank you for your support of Webot!
