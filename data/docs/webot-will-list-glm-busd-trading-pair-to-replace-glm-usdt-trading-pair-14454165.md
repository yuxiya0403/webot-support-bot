# Webot will list GLM/BUSD trading pair to replace GLM/USDT trading pair
Dear users,

Due to the poor liquidity of GLM/USDT, Webot will delist GLM/USDT trading pair on February 17th, 2023 (UTC). Users can trade GLM with GLM/BUSD trading pair.

**Please note**

- Webot will delist the GLM/USDT trading pair and cancel the related manual orders and bot orders at 08:00 on February 17th, 2023 (UTC), and the GLM positions will be released to the trading account.

- The rebalancing bot orders containing GLM assets will not be canceled, and the GLM positions in the orders will be released to the trading account.

- Webot will list the GLM/BUSD trading pair on February 17th, 2023 (UTC). Users can trade GLM with the GLM/BUSD trading pair after delisting the GLM/USDT trading pair.
