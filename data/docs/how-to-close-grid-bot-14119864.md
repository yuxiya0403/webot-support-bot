# How to close grid bot
> If you want to close the running grid bot, please follow the steps in the image below.👉🏻 Web operation step👉🏻 App operation stepWeb operat...
If you want to close the running grid bot, please follow the steps in the image below.

**👉🏻 **[**Web operation step**](#h_01H51TYHA5JFBQTBPPKNHSNTC5)

**👉🏻 **[**App operation step**](#h_01H51V25R8QWVEXSD7GG50JZHZ)

Web operation step

Open the [Webot](https://www.webot.com) webpage and log in to your account, then click **[Trade] - [Bot] - [Detail] - [Stop the Bot] **to close the bot.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177991457/5a19bae76b7de708dd72dc801c5f/20545610348697?expires=1776232800&signature=55e74f0270d4e3b2813ae06917a33e64c9884315d21f3f4cd734297c9e7fef4f&req=diEgEcB3nIVaXvMW3nq%2Bgc4OFCIRnknSvR9fr0AuSAOC0hqfnwSg0I%2BbHYaO%0AIaw3DmX2Cff1NzYZ7AN5L6Rw1DE%3D%0A)

App operation step

Open the app and log in to your account. Click **[Bot]**, find the bot order, and click **[Edit/Stop]** -** [Stop the bot]** to close the bot.
