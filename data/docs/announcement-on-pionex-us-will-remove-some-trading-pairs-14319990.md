# Announcement on Pionex.US will remove some trading pairs
> Dear users:In order to improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will remove the following trading pairs at 12:00 on Novemb...
Dear users:

In order to improve transaction depth and user experience, Pionex.US will remove the following trading pairs **at 12:00 on November 27th, 2022 (UTC)**:

- **SRM/USDT, SRM/BTC;**

- **TRIBE/USDT**

**Please note:**

- Pionex.US will automatically close SRM-related and TRIBE-related trading bots' orders **at 12:00 on November 27th, 2022 (UTC)**. If you are worried about price fluctuations, it is recommended to manually close the orders in advance.

- The SRM and TRIBE token assets will be retained in the Trade wallets after the corresponding bots are canceled. The rebalancing bots orders with SRM and TRIBE balance will not be canceled. The holding position of SRM and TRIBE will be released to the Trade wallets.

- Pionex.US will list SRM/BUSD and TRIBE/BUSD trading pairs as soon as possible after the SRM/USDT, SRM/BTC, and TRIBE/USDT trading pairs are delisted. Users can still trade SRM and TRIBE tokens through corresponding trading pairs.
