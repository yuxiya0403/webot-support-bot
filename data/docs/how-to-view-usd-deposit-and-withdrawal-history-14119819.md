# How to View USD Deposit and Withdrawal History
To view your USD deposit and withdrawal history, follow this guide:

**USD Deposit History**

To view the USD Deposit History on your mobile app and website, follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]**.

**Step 2:** Tap the **[History]** icon and select **[USD]** as your currency.

**Step 3:** Select the **[Debit Card]** filter to view your specific deposit records.

_

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to [Webot](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and navigate to **[Orders]** > **[Deposit USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select the corresponding channel on the left to view your specific deposit records.

_

**USD Withdrawal History**

To view the USD Withdrawal History on your mobile app and website, please follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]** and tap on **[Withdraw]**.

**Step 2:** Tap **[History]** and check the **[USD] **page to view your withdrawal details.

_

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to the Webot website and go to **[Orders]** > **[Withdraw USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select your preferred withdrawal method to view the specific transaction history.

_

Please get in touch with Webot Support if you have any other questions.
