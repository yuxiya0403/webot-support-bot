# How to View USD Deposit and Withdrawal History
To view your USD deposit and withdrawal history, follow this guide:

**USD Deposit History**

To view the USD Deposit History on your mobile app and website, follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]**.

**Step 2:** Tap the **[History]** icon and select **[USD]** as your currency.

**Step 3:** Select the **[Debit Card]** filter to view your specific deposit records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241134989/4aa836e83bc4203b3fe5cc18d2fe/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=1deea833ac775a47b952b125f27fbf2f05edad93636d2abe451f5c9a6f3e5487&req=diIjF8h9mYhXUPMW3nq%2BgQYqoUPcb6CqhMIX7p%2B8zr76cd8zpD4y0ukX%2FiTF%0AT1ff%2BirvACU%2FGAiP5qDPYm239YE%3D%0A)

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to [Webot](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) and navigate to **[Orders]** > **[Deposit USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select the corresponding channel on the left to view your specific deposit records.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241134990/f0ac5e72ae643f55ec4285740fa9/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=9c4e1f3a45ae0a5ee568124220d846e62f090b24c7577add772854abc2bfdf14&req=diIjF8h9mYhWWfMW3nq%2BgaUZsko38v4VA7dL6TUcyj9lE%2BkDJxFrNaheD2QB%0ARdKPL0GBfOzr24CEMc%2FqjUU9MiQ%3D%0A)

**USD Withdrawal History**

To view the USD Withdrawal History on your mobile app and website, please follow these steps:

**On the Mobile App**

**Step 1:** On the **[Home]** screen, tap on **[Deposit]** and tap on **[Withdraw]**.

**Step 2:** Tap **[History]** and check the **[USD] **page to view your withdrawal details.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2241134992/7d346317c7847cc207f3f7b7e2b6/image.png?expires=1776232800&signature=5cc1264ffac356aa3da7e896c9bd36e3f2a051d308fd2553d73e2fc3e2443aa8&req=diIjF8h9mYhWW%2FMW3nq%2BgTA6vfa0qrWLarkX1QiK%2BazkctJXaaWPqObd1jj5%0AwJbE4dMDwxqLzZ4L8BMIt%2B0e81E%3D%0A)

**On the Website**

**Step 1:** Log in to the Webot website and go to **[Orders]** > **[Withdraw USD orders]**.

**Step 2:** Select your preferred withdrawal method to view the specific transaction history.
