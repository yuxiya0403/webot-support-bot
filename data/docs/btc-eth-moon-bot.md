# BTC & ETH Moon Bot (BTC天地单 / ETH天地单)

## What is the BTC/ETH Moon Bot?
BTC/ETH Moon Bot is a pre-configured grid trading strategy for BTC or ETH. Parameters are automatically set based on current market prices — no manual configuration needed.

The strategy uses Webot's grid trading technology. As long as the price fluctuates within the predefined range, the bot runs 24/7, capturing grid profits from market movements.

Grid profits can be withdrawn at any time.

The strategy has been validated over two years of real-market operation, achieving a peak return of up to 21×.

## Profit Metrics
- **Total profit** = Grid profit + Unrealized profit (trend gains)
- **Grid profit** = Profit earned from buying low and selling high within the grid
- **Unrealized profit (Trend PnL)** = (Current price – purchase cost) × number of tokens held
- **Grid annualized** = [(Grid profit / Investment) / (Lasting time / 365)] × 100%

## How to Create (App)
1. Open Webot app → Bot → Create → Strategy
2. Select BTC Moon or ETH Moon
3. Choose token: USD or USDT
4. Enter investment amount → Continue

## How to Create (Web)
1. Log in → Trade → Trading Bot → Strategy
2. Select BTC Moon or ETH Moon
3. Choose token: USD or USDT
4. Enter investment amount → Create

## FAQ
**Q: What should I do if the bot shows a loss?**
A: The bot holds some spot positions which fluctuate with price. As long as you hold long-term, losses are expected to recover as price rebounds.

**Q: Is fund utilization low?**
A: The bot keeps some cash reserves for buying dips. This is intentional risk management — it allows the bot to buy more when price drops.

**Q: Will BTC or ETH go to zero?**
A: BTC and ETH are leading cryptocurrencies with long-term value. If you believe in the long-term growth of blockchain, these assets are unlikely to go to zero.

**Q: Can I add or reduce investment after creating the bot?**
A: No. Investment amount is fixed after creation. Close the bot and recreate it to change the investment.

**Q: Can I modify parameters while the bot is running?**
A: No. Parameters cannot be modified while the bot is running. Close and recreate to change settings.
