# Webot listed ETHW(EthereumPoW)
Dear users,

Webot has listed ETHW(EthereumPoW) and opened the ETHW/USDT trading pair.

Webot has completed the distribution of Ethereum Proof-of-Work (ETHW) tokens to eligible ETH holders, following the successful implementation of the Ethereum Proof-of-Work (ETHW) chain. 【The snapshot time for the ETHW distribution was taken on 2022-09-15 at 06:42 (UTC) - The ETH mainnet completed the merge and upgrade on 2022-09-15 at 06:42:42 (UTC).】Eligible users will be credited ETHW at a ratio of 1 ETH = 1 ETHW in their Trade wallets.

Please note:

- The snapshot will include the net ETH balances in Trade wallets and Earn wallets.

- The minimum precision of the distribution of ETHW tokens is 0.0001 ETHW. Users will not be credited ETHW if less than 0.0001 ETHW.

- Webot has listed ETHW(EthereumPoW) and opened the ETHW/USDT trading pair.

- Deposits and withdrawals for ETHW will be enabled on 2022-09-23 at 09:00 (UTC) (estimate only).

The process of ETHW(EthereumPoW) token distribution will be completed within **24 hours after this announcement **and can be viewed in the Deposit history record.
