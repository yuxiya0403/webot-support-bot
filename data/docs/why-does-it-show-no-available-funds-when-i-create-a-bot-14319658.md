# Why does it show no available funds when I create a bot?
> Reason 1   Holding "USD" but choosing a "USDT" trading pair

**Reason 1 Holding "USD" but choosing a "USDT" trading pair**

When you fund your Pionex.US account with your bank card, the token you hold in your account is "USD" and you select a "USDT" trading pair to create a bot. Since you did not select a matching token, the system will show that you have no available funds.

**Solutions**

If you are using the Web, you can switch to the **"USD"** trading pair at the location where you created the bot. For example, when the interface shows the **"ETH/USDT"** trading pair, you can switch it to the **"ETH/USD"** trading pair.

If you are using the app, you can switch the token from **"USDT"** to **"USD"** by clicking on the button in the picture.

**Reason 2 Holding "USDT" but choosing a "USD" trading pair**

When you fund your Pionex.US account with "USDT" from another exchange or wallet, the token you hold in your account is "USDT", but you select a "USD" trading pair to create a bot. Since you did not select a matching token, the system will show that you have no available funds.

**Solutions**

If you are using the Web, you can switch to the **"USDT"** trading pair at the location where you created the bot. For example, if the interface shows **"ETH/USD"** trading pair, you can switch to **"ETH/USDT"** trading pair.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009912/1784b9121d94899b3f2eb11f6431/10920059142297?expires=1776232800&signature=a2e4dcc654680348547d7bc397a53657f189c99847982540b27d07699e893bf5&req=diImHsl%2BlIheW%2FMW3nq%2BgcI9ONNAB4fSH0Xp%2FjDX1Bs%2FjoQhlh43gMAdsfNk%0AU6vRBl3wptNznlRd2bTypZ%2B2GIo%3D%0A)

If you are using the app, you can switch the token from **"USD"** to **"USDT"** by following the steps in the picture and clicking on the buttons.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009926/90067fcf65ba7191d6c7bc517057/10918475352985?expires=1776232800&signature=7794ee2120f866838ba5829ae465ccf7d55a6bb8ddf8ca873b2230b1e427f958&req=diImHsl%2BlIhdX%2FMW3nq%2BgZz1cj7ofbY08ouzh48hlbrGND6Bgxo4Uv5t0fBL%0A%2B5hXlTlT%2BVEJncyLP%2F43oTmxY%2B0%3D%0A)

**Reason 3 Unfilled limit orders lock funds**

You currently have unfilled limit orders in your account, which will result in your funds being locked and not available for creating a bot.

**Solutions**

You can unlock the funds by canceling the limit order.

If you are operating on the web, please click **"Trade" **- **"Manual" **- **"Manual" **, then click the **"CANCEL" **button on the corresponding order to cancel the order, and finally click the **"CONFIRM" **button.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009954/bd071979137475765ff734b897f5/10917905417241?expires=1776232800&signature=c6bf6b287af429365bfe3d64aea3ec5f6ef47d82f9deed906c6b6f0b919db243&req=diImHsl%2BlIhaXfMW3nq%2BgU6%2BHU254rxR5C6Yh%2BiYISipIP7%2FA2zXOPG1pDOW%0AEgCEGgSPIxEgOlbM00Eyl1KZHqU%3D%0A)

If you are operating in the app, please click **"Swap" **- **"Manual" **- **"All Pair"**, then click the**"Cancel" **button on the corresponding order to cancel it. Finally, click the **"CONFIRM" **button.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2218009999/331bcaf0cf10d4de68bd95f9dbd4/10917943330073?expires=1776232800&signature=17e0022aa38907212f733513127aae99b33ecb4500640042128ab02d5e9b118a&req=diImHsl%2BlIhWUPMW3nq%2BgWdDbNqeOz6UTc7G27bKKvBLoyr1hlMc42r8HGu7%0A4nY9gydqZG75NgQrZ4FHFYUY74M%3D%0A)

**Reason 4 Rebalancing bots can only be created using "USDT“**

Since you can only create rebalancing bots with "USDT" , you cannot create rebalancing bots with "USD".

**Solutions**

You can use the "SWAP" function to convert all the **"USD"** in your account to **"USDT"**, once you have successfully converted "USDT", you can create a rebalancing bot. [Please click here if you don't know how to use the "SWAP" function.](https://pionexus.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10648195791641)
