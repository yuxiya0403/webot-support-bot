# Pionex.US & Webot are now Two Separate Apps

Dear Users,

To provide a more streamlined downloading and trading experience, we are officially implementing a **Dual-App Strategy**.

Effective immediately, we are separating our services into two standalone applications to cater to different trading preferences.

- Pionex.US (The Classic Edition)
**The app currently installed on your phone will revert to the Classic Orange Interface.**

- **What it is**: The stable, familiar exchange interface you have trusted for years.

- **Best for**: Users who prefer the classic layout and manual trading tools.

- Webot (The New Brand)
Webot will be available as a separate download soon.

- **What it is**: Our next-generation platform featuring a professional blue interface and enhanced AI bot capabilities.

- **Best for**: Users who want to experience the latest UI innovations and automated AI trading.

One Account, Shared Access

Please note that these are two interfaces for the same secure platform.

- **Shared Login**: Use your existing email/password for either app.

- **Shared Assets**: Your funds and active bots are synced in real-time between both apps.

- **Same Security**: Both operate under the same US compliance standards (NMLS #2284360).

The Webot Team (Formerly Pionex.US)
