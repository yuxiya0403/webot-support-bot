[FTT deployment of smart contracts](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x048906570650e3c282e42832de167a0270c7890c77192362a0bff0f5bf415d3b)

- FTT (ERC20) depositing and withdrawing have been closed.

- FTT-related transactions will not be affected.
