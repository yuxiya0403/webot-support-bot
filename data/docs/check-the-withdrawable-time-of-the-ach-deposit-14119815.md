# Check The "Withdrawable Time" of The ACH Deposit
You can check your ACH deposit's "Withdrawable Time" by following the steps.

👉🏻 **[App operation step](#h_01KF2VX889KH1XY504SWJX63J0)**

**👉🏻 ****[Web operation step](#h_01KFJAEVD6QEC1K1ZM5DAFB3AG)**

**Mobile App operation step**

Please click **[Deposit] **- **[History] - [Bank Account]** to check the **[Withdrawable Time]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177993530/d9f22873cb4605724ee9b784cae0/14960288149647?expires=1776232800&signature=c5a8eaed362de19ae719da3782c20d15f4f507ff2815f3db1434411db8c5865c&req=diEgEcB3noRcWfMW3nq%2BgS1XsTT90aQRR5li7HDUbgmO4G0fJqVkWnS%2FMRKv%0AnccfEQ9ZNCXgYJ%2Bx14q2PV1WryM%3D%0A)

**Website operation step**

1. After logging in to your [Webot](https://www.webot.com/us/en-US) account, click **[Orders]** - **[Deposit USD orders]**.

![](https://webot-d125655f84fc.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/c6h9pild/2177993580/56b7d688445691a02492751a8965/14960266582671?expires=1776232800&signature=fb74b759c5358bb20ddaf788dae7da72bdc37993af0e9c268d6fa12f9163217c&req=diEgEcB3noRXWfMW3nq%2BgWUUSiRpqUcP4bJI6Td3MQWhFoDBj%2Bk8KL%2F6H9cc%0A5UeiuAbnOLbSm1FymW8XQM1oHPY%3D%0A)

2. Click **[Bank Account]** under the **"Deposit USD"** tab, and click **[ACH (Stripe)] **to check the** [Withdrawable Time]. **
